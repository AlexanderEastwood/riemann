\subsection*{Fixed translate spacing: conditioning and the remaining complement}
\label{ns46-a2-sec-lattice}
Let $\phi$ be the normalized even Gaussian arithmetic radical above,
with unitary Fourier transform $\widehat\phi(t)=c\Xi(t)$, $c\ne0$.
For fixed $d>0$, put
\[
 S_d(\theta)=\frac{2\pi}{d}\sum_{k\in\mathbb Z}
       \left|\widehat\phi\left(\frac{\theta+2\pi k}{d}\right)\right|^2.
\]
\begin{proposition}[Every fixed-spacing lattice has a positive Gram floor]
\label{ns46-a2-prop-lattice}
The continuous periodic function $S_d$ is strictly positive.
For every finitely supported coefficient vector,
\[
 \left\|\sum_jc_j\phi(\cdot-jd)\right\|^2
 =\frac1{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi}S_d(\theta)
             \left|\sum_jc_je^{-ij\theta}\right|^2\,d\theta.
\]
Consequently all finite lattice Grams lie between
$(\min S_d)I$ and $(\max S_d)I$, with $\min S_d>0$.
No quantitative lower bound as $d\downarrow0$ is asserted.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The identity is Plancherel followed by partitioning frequency into
intervals of length $2\pi/d$. Schwartz decay makes the periodization
uniformly convergent. If $S_d(\theta)=0$, all its summands vanish,
so $\zeta(1/2+i(\theta/d+2\pi k/d))=0$ for all integers $k$.
The gamma and elementary factors of $\Xi$ have no zeros on this
line. Li--Radziwi\l\l's unconditional Theorem 4
\cite{ns46-LiRadziwill2015} gives a positive asymptotic proportion
of nonzero zeta values on every fixed progression of this form,
a contradiction. Compactness of the circle gives the positive minimum.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Exact incompleteness.}
This infinite lattice nevertheless spans a proper subspace of
$L^2(\mathbb R)$. Put $\omega=2\pi/d$ and choose a measurable set
$E\subset(0,\omega)$ of positive measure. Set
\[
 \widehat v(t)=\widehat\phi(t+\omega),\quad
 \widehat v(t+\omega)=-\widehat\phi(t)\quad(t\in E),
 \qquad \widehat v=0\ \hbox{elsewhere}.
\]
The transform $\widehat\phi$ is real on the real axis and nonzero
almost everywhere, so $v\ne0$. Its pairing with every lattice
translate cancels between these two frequency bands, since
$e^{ijd\omega}=1$. Multiplication of both pieces by arbitrary
$L^2(E)$ functions gives an infinite-dimensional orthogonal complement.
Adding or subtracting the reflected construction gives nonzero even
and odd orthogonal vectors separately, since the positive and negative
frequency supports are disjoint. Thus both parity complements remain
infinite-dimensional.

For every fixed $d$ the v1.35 expanding central lattice construction
can therefore use this positive Gram floor, with constants depending
on $d$. It changes the coefficient of its $O(a)$ rank, not its order.
The fixed-$d$ nonvanishing theorem gives no justified constant for a
spacing $d=d(a)\to0$; the dense finite block instead uses the explicit
finite-arc estimate. None of these facts estimates the signed Weil
form on the remaining complement. Incompleteness is not a proof that
all problematic spectral directions escape the lattice span.
