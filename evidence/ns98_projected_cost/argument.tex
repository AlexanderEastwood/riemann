\section*{NS98: projected-cost attempt and stop record}

\paragraph{Outcome and overlap.}
No scale-independent average cost bound was obtained. The complete Schur
complement is already NS53/61/73, the signed-suffix budget is NS92, and
bounded-diagonal separation is already the method of NS93. This note
applies those identities to the actual projected cost, identifies the
unchanged missing estimate, and records two finite checks. It is an
attempt/audit, not a new arithmetic theorem or a new RH route.

\paragraph{Fixed normalization and complete cost.}
Use the notation of NS92, with
\[
 a_n(t)=A(t/n),\quad d_n=-\mu(n)\log(2N/n)\quad(N<n\le2N),
 \quad K_N=\|(I-\Pi_N)\sum_{n=N+1}^{2N}d_na_n\|^2.
\]
The prescription is not rescaled. Let $G$ be the old Gram, $C$ its
old/new block and $D$ the new Gram, all in the complete space
$L^2((0,\infty),dt/t^2)$. Set $Q=C^TG^{-1}C$ and $H=D-Q$.
The exact old correction is $-G^{-1}Cd$, so
\[
 K_N=d^THd=d^TDd-d^TQd.
\]
Both matrices $Q$ and $H$ are positive semidefinite; here $H$ is positive
definite by finite independence. This does not assign signs to their
individual off-diagonal entries or to their signed off-diagonal sums.
The displayed identity itself is not progress on a bound.

\paragraph{The signed term that remains.}
In physical-atom coordinates put
\[
 D_N^{\rm proj}=\sum_n d_n^2H_{nn},\qquad
 C_N^{\rm proj}=2\sum_{m<n}d_md_nH_{mn},\qquad
 K_N=D_N^{\rm proj}+C_N^{\rm proj}.
\]
Writing $\alpha=\log2$, $\kappa=\log(2\pi)-\gamma$,
NS61 gives $\|a_1\|^2\le4\kappa$ by $\|J\|=2$ and
$\|\rho_1\|^2=\kappa$. Dilation gives $\|a_n\|^2=\|a_1\|^2/n$.
Thus, for every integer $N\ge1$,
\[
 0\le D_N^{\rm proj}\le
 \sum_n d_n^2D_{nn}\le
 4\kappa\alpha^2\sum_{n=N+1}^{2N}\frac1n
 \le4\kappa\alpha^3.
\]
This is an elementary diagonal bound, not an upper bound for $K_N$.
In particular, the desired bounded means of $K_{2^j}$ on
$J\le j<2J$ are equivalent to bounded upper means of
$C_{2^j}^{\rm proj}$. That observation supplies no estimate of either.
These are not NS93's scalar $D_N,C_N$: the kernel $H$ includes the full
old refit. No equality between the two signed cross terms is asserted.

The new estimate attempted was an upper bound independent of $J$ for
\[
 \frac2J\sum_{j=J}^{2J-1}
 \sum_{2^j<m<n\le2^{j+1}}
 \mu(m)\mu(n)\log(2^{j+1}/m)\log(2^{j+1}/n)
 \left(D_{mn}-(C^TG^{-1}C)_{mn}\right).
\]
It remains unproved, including when $J$ runs only through sufficiently
large powers of two. Refitting gives a nonnegative subtraction from the
\emph{full} raw cost, but does not compare that subtraction to the raw
signed cross terms. A nonpositive projected cross sum would be one
stronger sufficient estimate. The finite tests below do not prove it.

\paragraph{Why the direct norm estimate returns to NS92.}
Let $F_N$ and $T_N$ be NS92's endpoint-corrected direction and signed
suffix. Boundedness of $I-\Pi_N$ permits the exact strong integral
\[
 (I-\Pi_N)F_N
 =\int_N^{2N}T_N(x)(I-\Pi_N)h(\,\cdot/x)\,dx.
\]
Replacing the projected generator norm by $\sqrt{\kappa/x}$ and applying
Minkowski gives precisely $K_N\le U_N\le\kappa\alpha S_N$ again.
This is a valid but previously established bound. Taking every Mobius
product's absolute value in the final $S_N$ returns to NS93's growing
majorant. NS93 does not prove divergence of $K_N$, nor does it exclude
an estimate that retains the projected signed kernel.

\paragraph{Finite scope.}
Complete Arb replays at 256 and 384 bits, with Bernoulli/Hurwitz tail
cutoff 128 and a separate cutoff-256 replay, give
\[
\begin{array}{c|ccc}
N&K_N&D_N^{\rm proj}&C_N^{\rm proj}\\\hline
16&(1.4234,1.4235)10^{-5}&(2.5649,2.5650)10^{-4}&(-2.4227,-2.4226)10^{-4}\\
32&(1.6087,1.6088)10^{-5}&(2.3465,2.3466)10^{-4}&(-2.1857,-2.1856)10^{-4}
\end{array}
\]
The projected costs overlap the prior NS73 enclosures. No new residual
target solve, gain fraction, best error or larger-size efficiency table
is supplied. The two negative cross sums are finite certificates only.
They establish no eventual sign, bounded average, asymptotic cancellation
or RH evidence. Complete Gram costs are also checked by direct assembly
of the old-corrected coefficient vector and by endpoint-correction
invariance; no infinite Gram tail is dropped.

\paragraph{Stop condition and next-step restriction.}
The attempt reached the same unestimated signed arithmetic input.
Do not continue this variant merely by renaming $C_N^{\rm proj}$,
enlarging the finite table, or invoking positive semidefiniteness to
discard cross terms. A further cost attempt needs an independent estimate
for the displayed signed quantity. Even such an estimate would still
need a compatible lower bound for the actual residual numerator, as in
NS91/93. This turn proves neither failure nor success of bounded projected
cost, and no impossibility of the PR42/NS87 route. Manuscript v1.66, RH/G2
and both historical missing-original evidence groups retain their status.
