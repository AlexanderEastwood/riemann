What changed in the lattice interpretation

The saved cutoffs are 9, 16, 36 and 64. The strict prime cutoff requires an endpoint term at the prime powers. The archimedean block cancels by the functional equation, without RH; the full zero sum and trivial-zero term remain, together with a Perron remainder for any finite prefix.

The exact lattice expression has varying cosine and sine envelopes. Equal crests and exact quarter-point zeros are not identities. At λ=3, the crest-minus-trough is 0.568698519867: −0.159765789563 from the full zero contribution, −0.003943883015 from trivial zeros, and +0.732408192445 from the strict endpoint. The naive sum was an amplitude contribution, not a crest-minus-trough.

The minorant result and its precise limitation

A packet in a negative region gives the valid bound η ≥ [(b+B)c − B]+, where B controls positive spill. A quantization gap instead gives η ≥ [cδ − Q]+, where Q is the packet’s original Weil energy. Neither subtraction may be dropped.

The manuscript proves explicit cosine/sine packet concentration and the cost of removing the source. For a half-cell at the stated carrier scale, the even concentration norm is below 0.509. The diagnostic 0.6–0.8 belongs to the whole negative set.

For K distinct minorant values, the proved conditional inequality is η ≥ [cDa/(2K) − Q]+. A linear level-count obstruction follows if an admissible bounded-energy packet sees a uniformly populated distribution of symbol levels throughout [−Da,0]. Those arithmetic and energy hypotheses are not supplied by Da → ∞ or the finite diagnostics. The nonpositive J-trough construction separately satisfies η ≥ dJ+1, with cofinal trough multiplicity still missing.

A two-value countermodel demonstrates the failure of the inference from depth and concentration alone. It is not the arithmetic symbol. The actual route remains blocked, not closed; the concentration criterion remains valid. The failure is in the proposed obstruction proof, not in the physical form.
