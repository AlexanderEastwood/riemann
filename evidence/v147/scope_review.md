# Internal scope and algebra review — NS-29

Classification: internal review of exact identities, proved implications and
named open inputs. This is not an independent audit or a numerical certificate.

- The global depth is positive D_a = -inf beta_a when beta has a negative
  part. D_(a,X) is a separate head-range depth. The family must prescribe N(a).
- The distribution hypothesis is for every band, not just every cumulative
  threshold. The affine counterexample satisfies the cumulative bound while
  omitting half the levels. It is explicitly not the arithmetic beta.
- Even/odd Fourier moduli are even. The factor two in c=2*b*alpha*kappa
  comes only from reflection. For theta<1, integration over a range theta*D
  gives theta^2, not theta, with density normalized by D.
- The minorant has a value -D. Inserting -theta*D adds no more than K gaps,
  because at least one of the K values lies at or below that endpoint.
- Upper packet density supplies an upper mass bound only. A lower density
  on an arbitrary positive fraction supplies no band intersection. Both
  lower density and coverage of every band are hypotheses in the proposition.
- The coarea identity is applicable because beta is nonconstant analytic:
  finitely many critical points on a compact frequency interval, with no
  positive-measure level sets. Endpoints and critical values have zero
  level measure. Interval domination extends to Borel sets.
- The zero field retains the endpoint and the entire trivial-zero sum.
  Its infinite Perron convention is inherited exactly from v1.46. A finite
  prefix is not covered by the trivial/endpoint correction bound.
- The correction bound is e_x + (4/5)*x^(-5/2)/(1-x^(-2)); each band must
  be shrunk at both ends. Small absolute error alone cannot establish an
  all-band assertion at arbitrarily fine level resolution.
- The derivative bound differentiates the actual finite prime expression;
  no RH or zero-term differentiation is used. The one-sided width at a
  boundary minimum is min(X,(d-t)/M), not twice that quantity. Range coverage
  plus the derivative bound gives only kappa_a=D/(M*X), with no uniform claim.
- The RH probe estimate follows from J >= -t*pi/a -(D-t)*weighted_mass,
  J <= -1/(6*pi), and 0<=w<=1. It may vanish at t=theta*D. Its normalization
  is unrelated to a physical packet; no change of probe class is implied.
- For the broad pulse, autocorrelation support is [-1/X,1/X]. Once 1/X<log2,
  every prime lag is outside support exactly; the continuum costs O(1/X).
  The archimedean integral is log X plus a finite constant. This is a
  construction failure, not a theorem excluding all broadband low-energy
  packets. The raw pulse is not claimed source-admissible.
- Source projection retains both a pointwise Fourier subtraction and the
  cross-term energy cost. The form domain and source operator domain are
  stated. Small L2 overlap alone is not small relative to X^(-1/2).
- Primary sources were checked on 2026-09-21. Riemann-von Mangoldt,
  zero-density, linear exponential-sum and pair-correlation estimates have
  different conclusions from ZLD. The assessment asserts no unproved
  logical independence, no equivalence to RH, and no exhaustive theorem
  nonexistence claim. ZLD's logical strength remains unclassified.
- Outcome: named open zero-distribution input AND independent bounded-energy
  packet coverage. Neither (a) nor (b) is removed. The route remains blocked,
  not closed. The weighted concentration criterion remains true as stated.
- No experiments or new windows, heads, tail metrics or zero enclosures.
  NS-28 belongs to Claude and is untouched. G2/RH and NS-1 recoveries stay open.

- The whole-head separation is sufficient, not necessary for v1.46. At
  fixed a, beta tends to +infinity, so the negative set is bounded and its
  normalized measure vanishes as X grows. No arbitrary-head uniformity.
