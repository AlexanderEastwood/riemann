The exact zero-field potential

Write ha=1(−a,a)h and Ih,a=∫−aacosh(x/2)h(x)dx. With the complete corrected zero field 𝒵a=βa, define Ya=ℱ−1(𝒵aℱha). Then, almost everywhere inside the window,

ra=(Ya−2Ih,acosh(x/2))/h,
Ta=Ya+2(Ih−Ih,a)cosh(x/2).

The finite-window pole mass matters. Substituting the global Ih into the first formula loses the exterior contribution. The inverse transform is defined in L²; the argument does not interchange a finite zero prefix, the height limit and an integral. Endpoint terms, trivial zeros, exterior edges and the odd pole remain present.

What had to change

The comparison must separate a growth clause from packet feasibility, finite-window constants from uniform ones, and general-measure counterexamples from assertions about the actual zeta function. “CAE cannot imply QG” would overstate the result: unknown CAE could be false. The proved cutoff statement instead refutes QG over all possible head rules independently of CAE’s truth.

The obstruction is the absent signed arithmetic estimate on every source-admissible even vector and every odd vector in the complete form domain. This is a missing bound, not a demonstrated negative Weil direction. Rewriting that bound has not proved it.
