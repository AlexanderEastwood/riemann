The gap that Proposition~\ref{prop:v121-cofinal-rh} isolates has a
classical name. In the screw-function realization of
Suzuki~\cite{Suzuki2026}, with $A_a$, $\lambda_a=\inf\sigma(A_a)$ and
the monotonicity \eqref{eq:g2-monotone} as above, let $g$ be the Weil
screw function and $a=\log\lambda$. A certified window
$\mathsf W_\lambda\succeq0$ is the statement $\lambda_a\ge0$, that is,
that the restriction of $g$ to $(-2a,2a)$ is a screw function on that
interval; in the finite sections this is positivity of a truncated
Hermitian moment problem whose parity sectors are the
Toeplitz-plus-Hankel and Toeplitz-minus-Hankel pencils $T(\phi)\pm H(\phi)$
of one symbol, in the sense of Basor--Ehrhardt~\cite{BasorEhrhardt2002}
(the exact splitting of the certified $\lambda=4$ matrix is recorded, as
a diagnostic, in \texttt{evidence/diag\_circle\_split/}). By the
continuation theorem of Kre\u{\i}n and Langer~\cite{KreinLanger2014},
$\mathsf W_4\succeq0$ says that the restriction of $g$ to
$(-2\log4,2\log4)$ admits at least one screw extension to $\mathbb R$;
RH says that $g$ itself is such an extension, so that the windowed data
continue to $g$ and to nothing else. In that sense RH is the
determinacy of the truncated moment problem, and determinacy at any
certified window would already be a statement of RH strength.
Positivity of finitely many sections never decides determinacy. This
is the content of Proposition~\ref{prop:v121-cofinal-rh} in language
that predates the present project: the cofinal estimate is the
determinacy statement, and no finite list of certified windows, however
long, approaches it. The paragraph names the wall; it does not move
it, and no G2 gap or RH claim follows.
