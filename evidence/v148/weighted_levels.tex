\subsection*{A level-measure-weighted form of the level-count bound (NS-30)}

Proposition~\ref{prop:v146-level-complexity} and the separation of
Proposition~\ref{prop:v147-level-separation} normalize the packet's level
mass against Lebesgue measure on $[-D,0]$. The arithmetic symbol does not
spend its negative range uniformly over levels: on the head range the
deepest tenth of levels carries a few parts in $10^4$ of the frequency
measure. Normalizing against the symbol's own level distribution instead
changes what the packet must supply and what the zero input must say.

Fix a window, the even sector, a head range $[0,X]$ with $X=2\pi N/L$,
and $D=D_a$. Define the normalized level distribution of the symbol and
the level mass of a packet by
\[
 \widetilde m(B)=\frac1X\bigl|\{\xi\in[0,X]:\beta_a(\xi)\in B\}\bigr|,
 \qquad
 \mu_g(B)=\int_{\{\beta_a\in B\}}|\mathcal F_ag|^2\,d\xi,
 \qquad B\subset[-D,0]\ \hbox{Borel},
\]
and put $\varphi_-=\widetilde m([-D,0))$. For $K\ge1$ define the
one-sided $K$-level quantization cost of $\widetilde m$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v148-qc}
 \mathrm{QC}_K(\widetilde m)
 =\min_{-D=\ell_1<\ell_2<\cdots<\ell_K\le0}\;
  \sum_{j=1}^{K}\int_{[\ell_j,\ell_{j+1})}(t-\ell_j)\,d\widetilde m(t),
 \qquad \ell_{K+1}=0.
\end{equation}

\begin{proposition}[Weighted level hypothesis and the quantization cost]
\label{prop:v148-weighted-levels}
Let $g\in P_a\mathcal H_a^{\rm ev}\cap\mathcal D(q_a)$ be a unit vector
with $q_a[g]\le Q$, $Q\ge0$, and suppose that for some $c'>0$
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v148-wlh}
 \mu_g(B)\ge c'\,\widetilde m(B)\qquad\hbox{for every Borel }B\subset[-D,0].
\end{equation}
Then every step minorant $m$ of \eqref{eq:v131-step-minorant} with at most
$K$ distinct values satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v148-weighted-loss}
 \eta(m)\ge\bigl[c'\,\mathrm{QC}_K(\widetilde m)-Q\bigr]_+ .
\end{equation}
If $\widetilde m$ has a density bounded by $\widetilde\rho_{\max}$ on
$[-D,0]$, then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v148-qc-lower}
 \mathrm{QC}_K(\widetilde m)\ge\frac{\varphi_-^{\,2}}{2K\,\widetilde\rho_{\max}},
\end{equation}
and when the level distribution is uniform on $[-D,0]$ this reproduces
\eqref{eq:v146-level-lower} with $c=c'\varphi_-$. Consequently, along a
cofinal family on which \eqref{eq:v148-wlh} holds with $c'\ge c_0>0$ and
$Q\le Q_*<\infty$, a bounded loss $\eta(m)\le C$ forces
\[
 K\ge\frac{c_0\,\varphi_-^{\,2}}{2\,\widetilde\rho_{\max}\,(C+Q_*)} .
\]
The level count is unbounded along the family exactly when
$\varphi_-(a)^2/\widetilde\rho_{\max}(a)\to\infty$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
A packet with $|\mathcal F_ag|^2\ge b/X$ almost everywhere on $[0,X]$
satisfies \eqref{eq:v148-wlh} with $c'=2b$, by evenness; the hypothesis is
stated in the weaker measure form. Let $-D=\ell_1<\cdots<\ell_n$ be the
distinct values of $m$ in $[-D,0)$, so $n\le K$, and put $\ell_{n+1}=0$.
The value $-D$ occurs because $m\ge-D$, $m\le\beta_a$, and
$\inf\beta_a=-D$; a minorant with fewer than $K$ negative values is
covered by monotonicity of the minimum in \eqref{eq:v148-qc} in $K$. Where
$\beta_a(\xi)\in[\ell_j,\ell_{j+1})$ one has $m(\xi)\le\beta_a(\xi)<\ell_{j+1}$,
so $m(\xi)\le\ell_j$ and $\beta_a(\xi)-m(\xi)\ge\beta_a(\xi)-\ell_j\ge0$.
Hence
\[
 \int(\beta_a-m)|\mathcal F_ag|^2\,d\xi
 \ge\sum_{j=1}^{n}\int_{[\ell_j,\ell_{j+1})}(t-\ell_j)\,d\mu_g(t)
 \ge c'\sum_{j=1}^{n}\int_{[\ell_j,\ell_{j+1})}(t-\ell_j)\,d\widetilde m(t)
 \ge c'\,\mathrm{QC}_K(\widetilde m),
\]
the middle step because domination of measures on Borel sets,
\eqref{eq:v148-wlh}, integrates nonnegative functions. The identity
$-\ip{T_mg}{g}=\int(\beta_a-m)|\mathcal F_ag|^2-q_a[g]$ of
Proposition~\ref{prop:v146-packet-loss} and $q_a[g]\le Q$ give
\eqref{eq:v148-weighted-loss}.

For \eqref{eq:v148-qc-lower}, let $m_j=\widetilde m([\ell_j,\ell_{j+1}))$.
A measure of density at most $\widetilde\rho_{\max}$ and mass $m_j$ on
$[\ell_j,\ell_{j+1})$ has $\int(t-\ell_j)\,d\widetilde m\ge m_j^2/(2\widetilde\rho_{\max})$,
attained by packing the mass against $\ell_j$. Then
$\sum_jm_j^2\ge(\sum_jm_j)^2/K=\varphi_-^{\,2}/K$ by Cauchy--Schwarz.
Under uniform density $\widetilde\rho_{\max}=\varphi_-/D$, and the right side
of \eqref{eq:v148-qc-lower} is $\varphi_-D/(2K)$. The cofinal statements
follow by rearranging \eqref{eq:v148-weighted-loss}.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{What changes in the open inputs.}
The zero-distribution input ZLD of Assumption~\ref{ass:v147-zld} asks for
a lower bound on the measure of \emph{every} band of levels. Under
\eqref{eq:v148-wlh} the only property of the symbol that enters is the
scalar $\varphi_-^{\,2}/\widetilde\rho_{\max}$, the negative fraction of the
head range squared over the peak level density; by
Proposition~\ref{prop:v146-beta-explicit} it is a functional of the
corrected zero field on $[0,X]$. The packet input changes from coverage of
every band with bounded energy to the existence of one admissible state
whose level mass is at least a fixed multiple of the symbol's own level
distribution, with bounded energy. Neither input is proved here; both are
named open inputs on the same footing as ZLD. The gain is in shape, not in
proof: the deep levels, which are rare on the head range, are now asked to
carry only their own share of the packet's mass.

\paragraph{Diagnostic values (not hypotheses).}
On the even head with $N=256$ and $X=800$
(\path{evidence/diag_level_distribution/}, sections~4--6, floating point),
the uniform hypothesis of Proposition~\ref{prop:v146-level-complexity} with
$c=0.3$ costs a minimum energy $Q$ of $5\cdot10^{-4}$, $10^{-3}$, $0.29$
and $0.13$ at $\lambda=3,4,6,8$, so \eqref{eq:v146-level-lower} has content
only for $K\le2$ at $\lambda=6$ and $K\le7$ at $\lambda=8$, below the
$13$--$15$ levels the measured minorants of
\path{evidence/diag_comb_minorant/} use. Under \eqref{eq:v148-wlh} with
$c'=1$ the minimum energy is $2\cdot10^{-9}$, below $10^{-15}$,
$1.8\cdot10^{-3}$ and $5\cdot10^{-5}$, the exact quantization costs are
$\mathrm{QC}_{16}(\widetilde m)=0.0031,\,0.0082,\,0.021,\,0.033$, and
$\varphi_-^{\,2}/\widetilde\rho_{\max}=0.028,\,0.11,\,0.31,\,0.39$. These
are head-resolved midpoint numbers; the source constraint is not imposed
on the minimizing states; they certify nothing and are recorded only to
show which shape of hypothesis the actual states satisfy. The route
remains blocked, not closed. G2 and RH remain open.
