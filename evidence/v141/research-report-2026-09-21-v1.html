What had to change

Exact identity. The matrix uses Fourier modes on [0,L]. Moving its cosine coefficients into the centered interval [−L/2,L/2] requires vn → (−1)nvn. The original NS-14 script omitted this factor and therefore evaluated a different function. Its sinc-lattice conclusion is withdrawn; the original files are preserved.

At a retained lattice point ωk = 2πk/L, the correct transform equals √(L/2)(−1)kvk. A small nonzero coefficient does not make that value zero. Low-mode mass concentration cannot establish the locations of transform zeros.
