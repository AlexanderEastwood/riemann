Where to spend the next effort

  1. Complete signed lower floor. Seek an arithmetic estimate valid on the whole even source complement and the whole odd sector, with one finite error independent of the window. This is the weakest sufficient cofinal target already proved in the repository. CAE and unrestricted exact adaptive concentration are equivalent ways to state it; neither is a new estimate.
  2. CCM relative selection. A concrete sufficient input is a complete second-eigenvalue separator above the explicit cutoff source's Rayleigh value, large enough relative to its residual to control the entire Fourier transform. An alternative is Rayleigh excess divided by the true complete gap, together with evaluator control or weighted tightness. A finite-compression gap does not provide this input. Global-ground use also requires odd-sector ordering.
  3. Arithmetic structure in joint concentration. Estimate the signed operator jointly, with the actual source removed. Even exact scalar concentration extrema for every set can miss its lower edge: two exact commuting countermodels have identical scalar data but signed bottoms −1/5 and +1/10. This refutes a general shortcut, not the actual arithmetic concentration route.
  4. QG plus packets, as an obstruction investigation. This could rule out bounded level complexity. It supplies no positivity mechanism, and packet feasibility remains separate. NS-40's literature assessment remains in PR #19.

Additional shortcuts now delimited

Deleting prime channels costs too much for this Gaussian weight. The new proof covers every fixed prime-power channel and omitted channels uniformly below the strict endpoint, including a bounded retained count or a cutoff o(exp(2a)). Exact even source-admissible and odd tests retain all cross terms. A fixed loss forces an unbounded ordinary-norm error. The conclusion requires unchanged other signed terms; coefficient-one, compensated, endpoint-approaching or different-weight estimates remain open.

A proposed small test family is not necessarily a weaker input. For an explicit smooth compact probe, bounded translated prime discrepancy would already exclude every off-line zeta zero. The proof uses the absolutely convergent von Mangoldt logarithmic-derivative identity, followed by analytic continuation and an explicit transform with only imaginary zeros. No such discrepancy bound is proved. For arbitrary probes, off-line zeros could still be masked at common transform zeros.

Absolute residuals do not select a ground profile. An exact abstract family has simple even nonnegative grounds with only real transform zeros, arbitrarily fast residual decay, ordinary resolvent collapse and tight ground support, yet its profiles alternate. The family is explicitly not the support-consistent arithmetic Weil family. A separate theorem rules out a nonzero single-profile limit only under its specified coarse-scale, nonnegative full-form liminf hypotheses. Finer arithmetic selection remains open.
