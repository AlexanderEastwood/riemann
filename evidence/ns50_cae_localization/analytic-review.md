# NS-50 internal analytic self-review

This is an author self-review, not an independent peer review or a certified computation. The reusable report is the separately versioned HTML file. Review baseline: v1.51, `b0baadf`.

1. **Object and sign check.** Independently expanding the off-diagonal kernel gives `K = -rho - sum w_n(delta_{ell_n}+delta_{-ell_n}) + 2cosh(t/2)`. Localization multiplies it by H, so the difference is `(H-1)K`. This agrees with the correlation derivation: plus archimedean and prime defects, minus full pole defect. The diagonal scalar cancels since H(0)=1. No parity pole was dropped.
2. **Normalization.** Unit L2 norm of chi gives `integral ||chi_s f||^2 ds = ||f||^2`. With the unitary Fourier convention, `c_f(t)=integral exp(i xi t)|fhat(xi)|^2 dxi`, so the multiplier has no extra 2pi factor. The factor four in the positive-half-line pole integral is two from the kernel and two from the two signs of t.
3. **Convergence.** `V(t)=||tau_t chi-chi||^2/2=O(t^2)` removes the `rho=O(1/t)` singularity. At infinity rho decays. Compact test correlation truncates the original prime and pole terms; compact H truncates their localized terms. The positive archimedean squared-difference formula justifies its localization before subtracting. All fixed-window domain constants may depend on a.
4. **Positivity and domain.** Every translated piece can be recentered inside lambda=4, so the inherited complete two-parity certificate supplies positivity. `m_chi=q[exp(i xi x)chi]` is nonnegative, real, even and continuous, and differs from the digamma multiplier by a bounded function. Thus its global square root is closed, unbounded, and has the logarithmic form domain. This is a property of the localized form, not a positive realization of global q.
5. **Obstruction.** The dense near-radical lemma controls the complete operator residual and ordinary convergence for each fixed vector in a dense linear span. Applying the proposed lower comparison gives bounded images in the closed square-root graph. Weak graph closure, lower semicontinuity of norm, and ordinary approximation extend a uniform operator bound from the dense linear span to all L2. The logarithmic multiplier contradicts this. No ordinary L2 density is being used to extend the unclosed Weil form itself.
6. **Parity and source.** Full-even and odd reflected translate spans are dense in their respective sectors. No corresponding density claim is made for the moving actual even source complements. The odd sector is already source admissible and is enough to defeat this proposed two-sector strategy. The local pieces need not satisfy a source condition because the local input is full positivity.
7. **Cofinal quantifier.** Support consistency and fixed chi make the full-parity best-error suprema nondecreasing. Each is finite because the remainder is bounded on a fixed window and theta L is nonnegative. Any finite cofinal bound would extend to every compact test and contradict the preceding argument. This proves divergence but no rate.
8. **Critical coefficient and capping.** At theta=1 the desired inequality is algebraically the original floor. At fixed multiplier cap M, existence of a bounded upper remainder is equivalent to existence of a floor, with constants shifted by at most M; no new bound has been supplied. Theta>1 leaves an unbounded negative positive-form term and does not alone imply an ordinary floor.
9. **Scope and dependencies.** This is a specialization of NS-47's closed-comparison principle, reproved here from v1.51. It neither disproves CAE nor excludes window-dependent localization or all arithmetic methods. The formal proof depends only on the pinned committed main manuscript, not the pending NS-47 or NS-46 integrations. No unpublished repository is used as external prior art.
10. **Review disposition.** No unresolved algebra/domain/source issue identified in this self-review. The full critical signed estimate is an explicit OPEN input. Independent mathematical review remains pending; no RH/G2 proof or publication-readiness claim is made.
