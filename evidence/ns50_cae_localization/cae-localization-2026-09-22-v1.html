RESEARCH CHECKPOINT · NS-50 · 22 SEPTEMBER 2026 · VERSION 1

Can local positivity give the cofinal CAE bound?

The tested localization argument does not prove CAE. Its proposed bounded error is impossible. The corrected estimate must retain critical signed cancellation; that estimate is still unproved. Nothing here proves G2 or RH, or produces a negative physical Weil direction.

Astra-3 took this task after Astra RH1 confirmed that no agent was actively deriving the full cofinal estimate beyond the earlier NS-34 and NS-47 checkpoints. The work is isolated on codex/ns50-cofinal-cae, based on v1.51, b0baadf. The earlier conclusions audit, PR #22, is unchanged.

The attempt

The existing λ=4 certificate says that every test supported in that window has nonnegative complete Weil energy. I tested whether an arbitrary larger test could be broken into translated pieces of that size, then reassembled with an error bounded by one constant times its ordinary squared norm.

Choose a real, even smooth cutoff χ supported in (−b,b), where b<log 4, with ∫χ²=1. Translate it continuously: χs(x)=χ(x−s). Define

ℒχ[f] = ∫ q[χsf] ds ≥ 0,
𝒟χ[f] = ℒχ[f] − q[f].

Exact identity: q[f]=ℒχ[f]−𝒟χ[f]. The localized pieces are positive after recentering because their supports fit inside the certified window. Both certified parities are needed: localization and translation need not preserve the parity or source orthogonality of each piece.

A uniform upper bound 𝒟χ[f]≤C‖f‖² would immediately give the desired full floor. This is the first candidate estimate tested here.

All cross terms, with their signs

Write cf(t)=∫f(x+t)f̄(x)dx, H(t)=∫χ(x+t)χ(x)dx and V(t)=1−H(t). With wn=Λ(n)/√n and ρ(t)=et/2/(2 sinh t), the exact remainder is

𝒟χ[f] = 2∫₀∞ρ(t)V(t) Re cf(t)dt
  + 2∑n≥2wnV(log n) Re cf(log n)
  − 4∫₀∞cosh(t/2)V(t) Re cf(t)dt.

The last term is the full pole contribution; its odd part has not been removed. Every prime shift is retained. For a compact test the correlations vanish beyond its support diameter, so the prime sum has the original strict cutoff. Near zero, V(t)=O(t²) cancels the archimedean singularity. The remainder is a bounded form at each fixed window; this says nothing about a common bound as the window grows.

The localized positive form has the exact Fourier multiplier

ℒχ[f] = ∫mχ(ξ)|f̂(ξ)|²dξ,
mχ(ξ)=q[eiξxχ(x)]≥0,
mχ(ξ)=log(2+|ξ|)+Oχ(1).

Proved implication: this defines a closed, positive, unbounded form in ordinary global L². Its unboundedness is the obstruction to the proposed lower comparison.

What fails, precisely

For every fixed 0≤θ<1, there is no finite C such that

𝒟χ[f] ≤ θ ℒχ[f] + C‖f‖²

for all compact smooth tests. The failure holds separately in the full even and odd sectors. In particular, θ=0 rules out the desired bounded localization error. The odd sector already satisfies the source condition, so it alone prevents this method from proving the two-sector CAE target.

The proof uses the established dense family of near-radicals: compact cutoffs ra converge in ordinary norm to each member r of a dense linear span, while q[ra]→0. The proposed estimate would bound (1−θ)ℒχ[ra] by C‖ra‖²+o(1). Closedness then forces the square root of ℒχ to be an everywhere bounded operator. Its logarithmically growing multiplier contradicts that conclusion.

This argument is a specific application of the NS-47 closed-comparison obstruction, not a new broad impossibility theorem. The proof is written out using only the already committed v1.51 dense near-radical lemma. It makes no assumption about RH. Even if RH is true, this stronger localization bound fails.

More precisely, the best constants for the displayed comparison, over unit tests in either complete parity sector, are finite at each fixed window and increase without bound as the support grows. No growth rate is proved. This is failure of the proposed bound, not failure of the physical form.

What had to change

The coefficient must reach the critical value one:

𝒟χ[f] ≤ ℒχ[f] + C‖f‖².

Open input: on the complete even source complement and the complete odd sector, with one C independent of the window, this is exactly CAE. The established source residual transfers it to a full uniform floor, which would imply RH. The localization identity supplies no proof of this signed inequality.

I also checked the immediate repair of capping the multiplier mχ at a finite value M. This removes the unbounded-factor obstruction, but existence of a uniform upper bound for the capped remainder is again equivalent to the original uniform-floor problem, with constants changed by at most M. The repair therefore provides no arithmetic estimate.

Changing the window with the localization scale, using a different completion, or proving a genuinely signed arithmetic inequality is outside this exclusion. None has been established in this checkpoint. In particular, an upper bound obtained by adding the absolute prime and pole terms would discard the cancellation the remaining inequality needs.
