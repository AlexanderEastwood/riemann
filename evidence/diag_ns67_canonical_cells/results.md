Numerical illustration only; not a certificate or an asymptotic bound.

NS-67 tests where the complete canonical error norm is concentrated and
how much cancellation occurs between the Abel boundary correction and
its integral plus target. The full signed formula is evaluated on integer
cells in t=1/x; a proved analytic tail is recorded, but ordinary floating
point runs are diagnostics. Finite tables cannot establish a cofinal
subpolynomial bound or a bounded subsequence.
