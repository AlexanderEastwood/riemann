What CCM ground selection still needs

NS-43 · 2026-09-22 · version 2 · complete-space selection subtask

A small absolute residual does not select the Gaussian radical. The useful next estimate is a complete spectral separation compared with the explicit source’s much smaller error. Entire-function convergence then needs a rate or weighted-tail estimate that survives the growing support. Neither estimate is supplied by the current fixed-window results.

This note introduces no numerical run, window, tail metric, certificate, G2 result or RH result. The full statements and proofs are in selection_note-v2.tex. All displayed implications below retain their stated hypotheses.

What is already available

The repository proves a simple complete even global ground at λ=4, and, under the checked real-zero theorem hypotheses, real zeros of its entire transform. Version 1.43 gives one complete-ground zero enclosure at that same window. Its transfer limitation is a limitation of the displayed bounds; it does not prohibit a different argument.

The explicit Gaussian arithmetic radical h is positive, even and rapidly decaying. Its normalized logarithmic profile φ=h/‖h‖₂ is known. Use the fixed v1.35 boundary profile: choose one smooth ϑ with 0≤ϑ≤1, ϑ(t)=1 for t≤−1 and ϑ(t)=0 for t≥−1/2, and set χa(x)=ϑ(|x|−a). Its derivatives of each fixed order are bounded uniformly in a. The normalized cutoffs pa=χaφ/‖χaφ‖₂ converge to φ in every exponentially weighted L¹ norm, and their complete residual obeys ‖Aapa‖≤εa=Ca exp(−ea/100), hence |αa|≤εa. This is exactly prop:v135-growing-radical / eq:v135-block-residual, because pa is its normalized central column. The estimate is not asserted for arbitrary cutoff families with growing derivatives. These cutoff vectors are not silently identified with the distinct repaired prolate source ua.

Versions 1.35–1.36 also provide a growing near-radical block, dense translated near-radicals, and ordinary strong-resolvent convergence of the zero-extended Weil operators to zero. The limiting zero operator has no selected direction. Those facts cannot themselves identify the ground with one prescribed radical.

A concrete sufficient selection estimate

Work on Ha=L²(−a,a), using the complete lower-bounded self-adjoint operator Aa with compact resolvent. In the even sector, odd ordering must be added separately when a conclusion requires the global ground. Let pa be the explicit unit cutoff above, αa=⟨Aapa,pa⟩, and ρa=‖(Aa−αa)pa‖.

Proved implication. Suppose an independent lower bound βa for the second complete eigenvalue, counted with multiplicity satisfies βa>αa. The ground is then simple. Choose its unit phase va to have nonnegative overlap with pa. Then

‖va−pa‖₂ ≤ √2 ρa / (βa−αa).

Proof: the trial gives μa≤αa<βa, so only the ground lies below βa. Decompose pa=κava+ea, with ea orthogonal to the ground and κa≥0. Spectral expansion gives ρa²≥(βa−αa)²‖ea‖². Since κa²=1−‖ea‖², the phase-aligned squared distance is 2(1−κa)≤2‖ea‖². The cross term vanishes because this is the actual spectral projection.

For R>0, put CR(a)=[(e2Ra−1)/(2πR)]1/2. Cauchy–Schwarz bounds the transform error on |Im z|≤R by CR(a)‖va−pa‖₂. Therefore the specific cofinal input

CR(a) ρa / (βa−αa) → 0 for every fixed R>0

implies locally uniform entire convergence to the transform of φ. This avoids needing a lower ground-energy estimate extraordinarily close to the source Rayleigh value. It still requires a complete cofinal separator at the correct relative scale. A finite-compression second eigenvalue, an LDL pivot, or a separately positive head/tail does not provide that separator.

Other sufficient ways to supply the missing control

Rayleigh-excess version. If μa is the simple complete ground, ga>0 is its complete spectral gap, and Ea=qa[pa]−μa, then

Ea=qa[ea]−μa‖ea‖² ≥ ga‖ea‖²,
‖va−pa‖² ≤ 2Ea/ga.

Thus Ea/ga→0 selects φ in ordinary L². Requiring CR(a)√(Ea/ga)→0 supplies the entire-function topology directly.

Weighted compactness alternative. The ordinary L² conclusion suffices if the actual grounds independently obey, for every R>0,

limK→∞ supa ∫|x|>K eR|x||va(x)| dx = 0.

On a fixed compact x-interval, L² convergence implies weighted L¹ convergence. This condition controls the remainder. A uniform bound on ∫e2(R+1)|x||va|² is sufficient. No such ground-tail statement is supplied by the present absolute residual.

Complete evaluator-dual alternative. Suppose Ea/ga→0 and, on the actual complete ground complement,

sup|z|≤R |F e(z)|² ≤ Da,R(qa[e]−μa‖e‖²),
Da,REa→0.

Then Fpa=κaFva+Fea, κa→1, proves the same locally uniform limit. This is a possible extension of the evaluator idea; the fixed-window v1.43 estimate for one real zero does not supply this cofinal complex-compact estimate.

A source-specific condition that retains the cross term

Let Qa=I−|pa⟩⟨pa| and ra=QaAapa. Assume the complete compressed form satisfies

qa[y] ≥ (αa+δa)‖y‖² for all y⊥pa, δa>0.

The second complete eigenvalue is at least αa+δa. For f=tpa+y, the full cross term is 2 Re(t⟨Aapa,y⟩). Completing its square gives μa≥αa−‖ra‖²/δa. The projected eigen-equation gives ‖va−pa‖≤√2‖ra‖/δa. No coupling has been discarded.

This is an additional source-specific spectral hypothesis, not an algebraic rewriting of the bounded-floor target. It asks for direction and relative-error information. An even-only estimate does not control odd parity; a full-space estimate or a separate odd floor is necessary to use the v1.36 floor reduction. No reverse implication from RH to this stronger condition is asserted.

The floor deduction from this condition does not require the relative selection rate: 2|t|‖y‖≤|t|²+‖y‖² gives qa[tpa+y]≥(αa−‖ra‖)(|t|²+‖y‖²). The known αa→0 and ‖ra‖→0 therefore give the sector floor directly. The relative rate is needed for the Gaussian profile and entire-transform selection.

The even reflected block from v1.35 has dimension Ja+1 and eventually contains a unit vector orthogonal to pa with complete residual at most εa=Ca exp(−ea/100). Hence δa≤εa−αa≤εa+|αa|=O(εa). A uniform or polynomial lower separation is ruled out. A much smaller separation could still select the source if the source error is smaller again. The archive proves no such hierarchy.

An exact abstract countermodel to selection from the weak premises

This model meets the premises listed below exactly. It does not impose the support-consistent arithmetic Weil form, prime identities, CvS distribution representation, or a positivity-improving semigroup. Its ground functions are nonnegative, not strictly positive everywhere in the growing interval. It is not an arithmetic disproof.

Let b=1[−1/4,1/4], let g₁ be the L²-normalized fourfold convolution b*b*b*b, and set g₂(x)=2−1/2g₁(x/2). These distinct nonnegative even unit functions lie in H², are supported in [−1,1] and [−2,2], and have entire transforms proportional to sinc(z/4)⁴ and sinc(z/2)⁴. Every transform zero is real.

On Hn=L²(−n,n), n≥3, let Dn be the complete Dirichlet Laplacian, and hn(x)=n−1/2cos(πx/(2n)) its unit ground. Choose g₁ for even n and g₂ for odd n, denote the choice by gj(n), and put

wn=(hn−gj(n))/‖hn−gj(n)‖,
Un=I−2|wn⟩⟨wn|,
An=n−1Un(I+Dn)Un.

The denominator tends to √2. This unitary reflection preserves the operator domain and parity, and sends hn to gj(n). Hence each An is positive, self-adjoint with compact resolvent, and has a simple even ground exactly equal to gj(n). Its ground energy is μn=n−1(1+π²/(4n²)), decreasing to zero, and its positive gap is 3π²/(4n³).

For every fixed compact smooth f, ‖(I+Dn)wn‖ is uniformly bounded, and

‖Anf‖ ≤ n−1(‖f−f″‖+2‖f‖‖(I+Dn)wn‖) → 0.

The same holds for the fixed candidate g₁. It also holds for the explicit Gaussian cutoff pn: its H² norm is uniformly bounded, so ‖Anpn‖=O(1/n), while its weighted limit remains φ and its transform limit remains proportional to Ξ. Zero-extending An gives ordinary strong-resolvent convergence to zero: the resolvent error on each fixed f is at most ‖Anf‖/(|z||Im z|), and density plus the self-adjoint resolvent bound handles all of L².

Thus the model has simple grounds, real transform zeros, positive monotone ground energies tending to zero, vanishing candidate residual, dense near-radicals, and strong-resolvent collapse. Its ground supports are uniformly bounded, so every exponential weighted-tightness requirement also holds. Nevertheless the normalized ground functions and normalized entire transforms alternate between two different limits. The failure is selection, not fixed-window eigenvalue multiplicity or loss of compactness. Replacing the factor 1/n by any positive θn↓0 preserves the grounds and makes every displayed residual O(θn). An arbitrarily fast absolute residual rate still cannot replace relative information. This rescaling does not restore arithmetic support consistency.

Separately, ordinary L² convergence alone need not give entire convergence. Add an even positive bump near ±(a−1) to the positive cutoff φ with coefficient e−Ra/2 and normalize. The vectors converge in L², while their transforms at iR grow like eRa/2. This separate topology example does not claim the real-zero property or the arithmetic eigen-equation.

A restriction on renormalized profile limits

A nonzero rescaled profile form could in principle distinguish the ground, but ordinary zero-resolvent collapse provides no such form. For this subsection only, qa denotes the full both-parity Weil form on L²(−a,a), zero-extended into the full H=L²(ℝ), rather than the even-sector form of the preceding selection statements. The scalar αa still belongs to the even cutoff source. This is the full-space setting of the dense translated-radical lemma. There is a precise restriction on a coarse scale sa: suppose εa/sa→0 and |αa|/sa→0, and suppose a nonnegative lower-semicontinuous quadratic form E satisfies the proposed strong liminf inequality

E(f) ≤ liminf (qa[fa]−αa‖fa‖²)/sa whenever fa→f in L².

Insert the known cutoffs of any fixed finite translated-radical combination. Their complete residual makes the right side zero. Such combinations are dense, so lower semicontinuity forces E≡0. Therefore a profile satisfying these hypotheses cannot have only the Gaussian ray as its kernel. This closes only that coarse-scale profile inference, not finer scales or arithmetic selection.

Identification and recommended status

The existing exact Mellin identity fixes the target: with the unitary Fourier convention,

Fφ(z)=2 Ξ(z)/(√(6π)‖h‖₂).

Once selection is proved, Fva(z)/Fva(0) converges locally uniformly to Ξ(z)/Ξ(0). The denominator is nonzero eventually because Fφ(0)>0. If the full real-zero-ground-transform theorem also applies cofinally, an off-real zero of Ξ would force zeros of the approximating transforms in a small off-real disk, contradicting their real-zero property. This explains the conditional endpoint; none of its missing cofinal inputs is proved here.

  • CCM step (b): retain “live”, the sole gold map node. The mathematical target remains open. Retain the fixed-window facts; require complete cofinal profile selection and the entire-transform upgrade.
  • Gaussian source identity and residual: proved prerequisites. They specify a candidate, not the operator’s ground.
  • Residual/resolvent-only selection: closed as an abstract inference. The countermodel defeats the listed weak premises. It does not close the arithmetic CCM route.
  • Relative spectral/evaluator selection: blocked/open input. Complete cofinal separators, relative errors, or a suitable ground-tail estimate are absent.
  • Coarse rescaled profile selection: closed only under the displayed scale and liminf hypotheses. Finer scales remain open.

For the actual support-consistent Weil family, the complete ground energy means μafull=min(μaeven,μaodd) on the full L²(−a,a). A uniform lower bound on this both-parity ground is already the v1.36 bounded-floor target; μafull→0 is equivalent to full both-parity positivity using monotonicity and the known source upper bound. No even-only equivalence is asserted. Those are not weak new assumptions. This note does not show that RH fails to imply arithmetic ground selection, or that an arithmetic selection theorem is impossible.
