Table~\ref{tab:ns4b-coverage} records which of the ten closed routes are
instances of the two scoped obstructions, checked against their
hypotheses rather than by analogy. Theorem~\ref{thm:v139-probe-relaxation}
covers exactly one of the ten, the scalar signed primitive: its budget
$a\Delta(\beta_a)$ is sound against every nonnegative unit density of
variation at most $2a$, a class that contains the probe
\eqref{eq:v139-probe-density}. The primitive is not covered by
Theorem~\ref{thm:v139-protected-orbit}, because the Bernstein bound
$\norm{F'}_2\le a\norm f$ is a property of the Paley--Wiener position
that the orbit destroys; the pointwise minimum, which is not one of the
ten, lies in both classes. The remaining nine
closures are covered by neither theorem. Five of them are stated on a
finite-codimension Fourier tail, outside the full-space or rank-one
hypothesis of Theorem~\ref{thm:v139-probe-relaxation}; the unsigned
prime-norm and far-majorant closures diverge for the elementary
unconditional reason $\norm{\mathsf T_{\rm pr}}\sim\lambda$ rather than for
the conditional probe pairing; the Schatten, block-metric, commutator and
Cotlar closures are losses of coupling inside a fixed physical partition
or Paley--Wiener kernel, which neither relaxation models; the flat-top
result is a rigidity theorem, not an estimate. The live criterion
\eqref{eq:v131-weighted-concentration} lies outside both classes for the
reason that matters: its operators
$\mathcal C_{a,u}(E)=P_a\mathcal F_a^*1_E\mathcal F_aP_a$ are sound
exactly on the physical densities, which exclude the compactly supported
probe, so hypothesis (A2) fails; and the adversarial rotation of one
physical vector into a sublevel band makes its weighted good-set mass
vanish, so the bound is not orientation-universal and hypothesis (B3)
fails. Were either theorem to cover it, the conclusion would contradict
the certified $\mathsf W_4\succeq0$ at a window where
$\operatorname*{ess\,inf}\beta_4<0$. The counterexample in
Proposition~\ref{prop:v139-location-counterexample} separates the two
classes: for $b_a$ the mass-cap budget is $+\tfrac12$ although the
orientation-universal edge is $-D_a\to-\infty$, and its class contains
the probe, so the arithmetic input of
Proposition~\ref{prop:v137-conditional-mass} is essential to
Theorem~\ref{thm:v139-probe-relaxation}. None of this changes the open
obligation: the cofinal signed concentration inequality with one uniform
finite constant in both parities.
