RIEMANN RESEARCH RECORD · NS-47 · 21 SEPTEMBER 2026 · VERSION 1

An exact geometric representation.
A uniform bound still missing.

The complete Weil form has an explicit Clifford representation, but this construction does not yet yield a lower bound uniform across growing windows. The work proves a precise obstruction to obtaining that bound from one global ordinary-closable Dirac square.

This is an analytic research checkpoint. No new window, tail metric or numerical certificate was computed. G2 and RH remain open. The task remains open at the uniform-bound objective.

What was constructed

Using the positive arithmetic weight already proved in v1.50, define D to collect every continuous and prime-shift squared difference. Define N to collect the centered variance and the negative odd-sector pole. On every compact smooth test, the exact identity is

q[f] = ‖Df‖² − ‖Nf‖².

The proof gives an explicit Cl(1,1) module for this signed pairing and a self-adjoint graded Dirac operator for the positive edge energy. Its square represents that positive energy; the negative channel remains part of the complete Weil form. The coefficient space is infinite dimensional.

All exterior edges remain in the representation. Even tests retain their actual source constraint. Odd tests retain their negative pole. The centering in the variance is not substituted for source orthogonality.

The precise missing estimate

The target becomes one inequality, with the same finite constant C for the growing windows:

‖Nf‖² ≤ ‖Df‖² + C‖f‖².

Equivalently, the negative channel must factor through (Df, √C f) by a contraction. This equivalence is proved, but the required contraction is not constructed. It is exactly the existing critical arithmetic energy comparison (CAE) in new coordinates.

The negative channel itself is unbounded as the window grows: the archived v1.50 admissible tests give ‖Na‖² ≥ [κ exp(γλ²) − Ba]+, with explicit κ, γ > 0 and Ba = O(λ log λ). The same tests exclude a uniform strict contraction factor below one. Local contraction norms may be below one, but their deficits must vanish at the displayed rate. The remaining problem is the exact cancellation, not a separate bound on the negative channel.

What had to change

A single global positive square plus a bounded remainder is unavailable if the square factor is closable in ordinary L². More precisely, no Hilbert-valued linear map T, closable on the compact smooth core, and bounded self-adjoint B satisfy

q[f] = ‖Tf‖² + ⟨Bf, f⟩.

The argument uses the already proved dense family of cutoff radicals. For each vector in that dense span, compact approximants converge in ordinary norm while their complete Weil energy tends to zero. A bounded remainder therefore bounds the images under T. Weak closure of the graph and density force T to be bounded on the whole ordinary space. The proposed identity would then bound q above. But normalized cosine and sine modulations on a single fixed compact support have q[fR] = log R + O(1), a contradiction.

The proof works separately in both complete parity spaces. A weaker one-sided comparison, q[f] ≥ ‖Tf‖² − C‖f‖², also forces any ordinary-closable T to be bounded, with norm at most √C. Thus an unbounded global Dirac factor cannot provide even this lower comparison.

This is a failure of the specified construction, not a negative Weil direction or a disproof of the lower bound. Fixed-window factors, families that do not combine into one global ordinary-closable factor, and different metric completions are outside the obstruction. They still require the uniform estimate and an explicit transfer to the ordinary norm. A successful full global contraction factor would necessarily give a positive square that is nonclosable in ordinary L².

References and next viable work

The block operator is a standard construction; see Holzmann (2021), Appendix A, Proposition A.1. The range-contraction argument is the elementary mechanism behind Douglas (1966). The arithmetic identity, source-admissible tests, and dense cutoff radicals come from the repository’s v1.50 and v1.36 proofs. No worldwide novelty is claimed for the general operator constructions.

The next viable attempt must work at the critical coefficient: construct a window-dependent or globally nonclosable cancellation factor and prove its ordinary error is uniformly bounded. Repackaging the same signed identity, or proving that the positive edge block is self-adjoint, is insufficient. This checkpoint narrows the allowable construction; it does not settle the arithmetic estimate.
