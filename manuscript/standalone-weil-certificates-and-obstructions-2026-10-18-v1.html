Certified Semilocal Weil Positivity at λ = 4, Ground-State Zero Localization, and Cofinal Obstructions for Fixed-Window and Nyman–Beurling Approximations

Alexander Eastwood

October 18, 2026 · Standalone Short Paper (v1) · Companion PDF: manuscript/standalone_weil_certificates_and_obstructions_v1.pdf

Abstract

We establish unconditional computer-assisted spectral certificates and analytical cofinal obstruction theorems for two Hilbert-space approaches to the Riemann Hypothesis (RH): the semilocal Weil quadratic form qa[f] = ⟨Wλf, f⟩ on L2(−a, a) (a = log λ) and the smoothed Nyman–Beurling–Báez-Duarte approximation problem. First, using validated ball arithmetic (flint.arb) at up to 1024/1280-bit precision, we prove complete positivity W3 ≽ 0 and W4 ≽ 0 across both parity sectors on the full physical form domain, certify that the complete λ = 4 even ground state ψ0 is simple with 0 < μ0(4) < 2.454 × 10−75 and μ1ev(4) > 10−73, and deduce via the Connes–van Suijlekom theorem that its entire Fourier transform has only real zeros, with its first positive zero enclosed within 8.752082 × 10−33 of the first Riemann zeta ordinate γ1 ≈ 14.13472514. Second, we prove that the uniform bounded-floor condition qa[f] ≥ −C*‖f‖2 along a cofinal sequence a → ∞ is both necessary and sufficient for RH without a positive spectral gap, and we rule out a broad hierarchy of proposed cofinal mechanisms: at λ = 5 the unchanged tail comparison and N = 25 dyadic block-norm comparison fail in both parities; achieving a uniform positive spectral gap by finite subspace removal requires removing rank Ω(λ2 / log λ); the optimal Bernstein signed-primitive drawdown diverges as aΔ(βa) ≥ ((log 2)/(3√2))a − C → ∞; any fixed relative slack δ0 > 0 or prime-2 channel deletion in the Gaussian positive-weight Picone identity forces an exp(γλ2) error blowup; and the prime-shift operator violates local open-set unique continuation and ordinary-cone semigroup positivity. Third, in the Nyman–Beurling lane, we construct an explicit inner-factor countermodel sharing the exact Nyman–Beurling Gram matrix while maintaining a strictly positive asymptotic error floor E∞(β) > 0, and we prove that any critical-line zero forces EN(q) ≥ (Cq + o(1)) / log N at every fixed smoothing order q ≥ 0, excluding eventual fixed-fraction contraction at every dyadic doubling. Every computational claim in this paper is backed by byte-verified, dual-precision interval certificates in the accompanying repository.

1. Introduction and the Semilocal Weil Form

Let λ > 1 and a = log λ. On the finite logarithmic interval Ia = (−a, a), decompose L2(Ia) = L2ev(Ia) ⊕ L2odd(Ia) into even and odd parity sectors. For a test function f ∈ L2(Ia) with zero extension f̃ ∈ L2(ℝ) and unitary Fourier transform f̂(ξ) = (2π)−1/2 ∫−aa f(x)e−iξx dx, the canonical semilocal Weil quadratic form qa[f] = ⟨Wλf, f⟩ is defined on its maximal closed form domain D(qa) ⊂ L2(Ia) by

qa[f] = ∫ℝ α(ξ) |f̂(ξ)|2 dξ − ∑2 ≤ m < λ2 (Λ(m)/√m) ∫−aa (f̃(x + log m) + f̃(x − log m)) f(x) dx + Π[f],

where α(ξ) = Re ψ(1/4 + iξ/2) − log π is the archimedean digamma multiplier, Λ(m) is the von Mangoldt function, and the polar contribution at s = 0, 1 splits by parity as the rank-two form

Π[f] = 2|⟨f, c⟩|2 − 2|⟨f, s⟩|2,   c(x) = cosh(x/2),   s(x) = sinh(x/2).

As λ → ∞, the classical Gaussian completed-zeta radical h(x) = ex/2 ∑n≥1 H(nex), with H(u) = (4π/√3)u2(2πu2 − 3)e−πu2, induces a normalized boundary-repaired even source vector ua ∈ D(qa) whose operator residual ‖Wλua‖ decays super-exponentially. Nevertheless, a positive spectral gap is not required for RH:

Theorem 1.1 (Cofinal Bounded-Floor Equivalence, evidence/v136/). The Riemann Hypothesis holds if and only if there exist a finite constant C* < ∞ and an unbounded sequence ak → ∞ such that
qak[f] ≥ −C*‖f‖2L2(Iak)
for all even f ∈ PakD(qak) (where Pa = I − |ua⟩⟨ua|) and all odd f ∈ D(qak) ∩ L2odd(Iak).

4. Nyman–Beurling / Báez-Duarte Reductions and Critical-Zero Floors

Theorem 4.1 (Same-Gram Positive-Floor Countermodel, NS-74; evidence/v161/ns74/). For any β > 1/2, multiplying the target Mellin transform by the unitary inner factor Iβ(s) = (s − β)/(s − (1 − β)) preserves the exact Nyman–Beurling Gram matrix Gm,n and all finite-prefix preconditioner efficiency ratios at every truncation N, yet maintains a strictly positive asymptotic squared error floor E∞(β) ≥ 1 − ((1 − β)/β)2 > 0.

EN(q) ≥ (Cq(ρ0) + o(1)) / log N   (N → ∞),
where Cq(ρ0) = 2 / (|ρ0|2 |1 + ρ0|2q |ζ'(ρ0)|2) > 0 (at q = 2, C2(ρ1) ∈ [7.785058046 × 10−8, 7.785058047 × 10−8] at 256/384 bits). Consequently, lim infj→∞ Δ2j / E2j(q) = 0; no uniform fixed-fraction contraction at every dyadic doubling is possible.
