Abstract. We present the complete, self-contained mathematical proofs and dual-precision interval certificates for the fixed-window Weil positivity theorems, simple ground-state and real-zero characterizations, and cofinal structural obstructions for the canonical semilocal Weil quadratic form $QW_\lambda$ on $L^2(-\log\lambda,\log\lambda)$ and its Nyman–Beurling / Báez-Duarte counterparts. First, we establish the closed self-adjoint realization $\mathsf{W}_\lambda$ of the semilocal Weil form on its logarithmic Sobolev core, prove the compact-source radical passage that eliminates point-value boundary terms on the exact translated radical $\mathcal{R}_\lambda^{\mathrm{tr}}$, and show that cofinal lower-boundedness $\mathsf{W}_{\lambda_j}\succeq -M I$ on the source-orthogonal complement $P_{\lambda_j}\mathcal{H}_{\lambda_j}$ along a sequence $\lambda_j\to\infty$ is equivalent to the Riemann Hypothesis (via dense-radical ordinary resolvent collapse). Second, using signed Schur complements, parity-split structured low-mode inverses, and outward Arb interval arithmetic at $256/384$, $320/448$, and $1024/1280$ bits, we prove unconditional complete positivity $\mathsf{W}_\lambda\succeq 0$ at $\lambda=3$ and $\lambda=4$ in both parities with exact fixed-window error $\varepsilon_4=0$, establish that the complete ground state $\psi_{0,4}$ at $\lambda=4$ is simple and even with $0 < \mu_0(\mathsf{W}_4) < 2.454\cdot 10^{-75}$ and $\mu_1(\mathsf{W}_4) > 10^{-73}$, deduce via the Connes–van Suijlekom theorem that its entire Fourier transform $\widehat{\psi}_{0,4}$ has only real zeros, and certify a simple sign-changing zero of $\widehat{\psi}_{0,4}$ in $[14.13,14.14]$ within $5.52\cdot 10^{-3}$ of the first Riemann zero $\gamma_1\approx 14.13472514$. Third, we prove a comprehensive suite of cofinal no-go theorems ruling out candidate continuation mechanisms as $\lambda\to\infty$: exponential rank growth $\exp(\kappa_\lambda/(1-c))$ for unsigned diagonal majorants; certified failure of the $\lambda=4$ tail metric and three-block dyadic norm-comparison criterion at $\lambda=5$ (NS-44/45); non-vanishing source-compressed concentration commutators and disjoint-channel cross terms; divergence of the arithmetic scalar primitive-drawdown budget $\sqrt{a}\,\Delta(\beta_a)\ge c_0\log a-C_0\to\infty$; fine translated-radical rank barriers $\ge \lambda^2/(20000\log\lambda)$; boundary-free dual-pole subspace equivalence and closed-form Bessel odd-sector pole divergence $2\pi a\,I_1(a)/(I_1(a)-I_0(a)) < -2\pi a$; and Nyman–Beurling / Báez-Duarte canonical sharp-cutoff, same-Gram countermodel, and critical-zero $\Omega(1/\log N)$ decay-floor obstructions. All fixed-window results are unconditional theorems; the cofinal uniform positivity margin (Goal G2) and the Riemann Hypothesis remain open.
