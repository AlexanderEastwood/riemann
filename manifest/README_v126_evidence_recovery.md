## Checks performed during publication

The ZIP integrity check and all extracted-file comparisons passed. Using
python-flint 0.9.0, the following original proof gates were replayed with
certificate writes disabled:

- `complete_margin.py` at 768 and 896 bits: both passed the complete even
  rational relative-margin gate `62629/100000` from the saved ingredients.
- `direction_complement_certificate.py` at 768 and 896 bits: both passed
  the `1/1000` complement bound. The latter reuses the original 768-bit
  ingredient enclosures, as documented by the original verifier.
- `certify_odd_full_window.py`: passed witness/report hash binding, head
  invertibility, exact rational equality of the physical head, and the
  directional inequalities reconstructed from the saved 1024/1280-bit
  reports. The combination gives the stated odd relative bound `107/250`.

These checks consume the original saved complete residual ingredients.
They are not a fresh replay of every residual row through J=65536 or an
independent derivation of the analytic kernel. No trial was generated and
no saved certificate was overwritten.
