What the checks established

The same pipeline accepted the Gaussian positive control and detected a negative form for an entire, pole-free control. This checks that the test can find a failure the previous pole search would miss.

A separate implementation then integrated the original theta terms with a different method at exactly the same six arguments. The largest observed relative quotient discrepancy was below 3.85e-69, and the same rational form stayed positive. This supports the stability of these sampled values.

Diagnostic, not an interval certificate. Complete lattice and integration-tail formulas are retained. Numerical integration, rounding and ratio errors are not enclosed. The decision

End this screening round. Shelve further quotient falsification screens until independent analytic input appears. This result gives no basis for a larger stencil or another spacing scan.

General positive definiteness of C/M remains open. So does the original first-Laguerre inequality. Even a negative quotient witness would have rejected only a sufficient construction; this positive finite screen proves neither that construction nor the original target.
