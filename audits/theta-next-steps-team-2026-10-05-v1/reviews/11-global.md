## Theta is a partial target, not the default winner

```text

J(r)=p(r)²(Y'(r)²-Y(r)Y''(r))+2(r²-1/4)Y(r)².
```

The first target is `J(r)>=0` for **every real r**, including zeros of Y, where the undivided formula stays valid. It equals `16 L1[X]`; first-Laguerre positivity alone is not RH. Any proposed proof must identify a separate subsequent bridge before being described as an RH route completion.
