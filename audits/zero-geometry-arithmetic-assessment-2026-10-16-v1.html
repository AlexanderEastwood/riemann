2. Five-Item Assessment of Candidate Mechanisms

Candidate I — Integrated Theta-Kernel Polarization & Autocorrelation Domination (NS100/101)

1. Exact expression With $f_x(u) = e^{xu}\phi(u)$, $b_x(u) = u\,f_x(u)$, and autocorrelation $\mathcal{A}[q](t) := \int_{\mathbb{R}} q(p+t)q(p-t)\,dp$, the integrated kernel $K_x(t) = \tfrac{1}{4}\partial_x J_x(t)$ ($J_x = \mathcal{A}[f_x]$) admits the exact polarization decomposition (NS101 Eq. (4)): $$K_x(t) = \frac{1}{8}\bigl\{\mathcal{A}[f_x + b_x](t) - \mathcal{A}[f_x - b_x](t)\bigr\}, \qquad \int_{\mathbb{R}} K_x(t)e^{2iyt}\,dt = \frac{1}{4}\operatorname{Re}\bigl\{F'(x+iy)\overline{F(x+iy)}\bigr\}.$$
2. Coefficient hypotheses Exact one-lattice Jacobi theta series $\Theta(r) = \sum_{n\in\mathbb{Z}} e^{-\pi n^2 r}$, modular reciprocity $h(t) = e^{t/2}\Theta(e^{2t}) = h(-t)$, differential annihilation of the $n=0$ mode via $\phi(t) = \tfrac{1}{4}(\partial_t^2 - \tfrac{1}{4})h(t) > 0$, and double-exponential decay $0 < \phi(t) \le \frac{2\pi^2}{1 - 16e^{-3\pi}}e^{9t/2 - \pi e^{2t}}$ ($t \ge 0$).
3. Uniform range All $0 < x < 1/2$ and all vertical heights $y \in \mathbb{R}$ inside the critical strip (with $y \to \infty$).
4. Estimating method Either (a) establish Fourier-side domination $\widehat{\mathcal{A}[f_x+b_x]}(2y) > \widehat{\mathcal{A}[f_x-b_x]}(2y)$ from the modular completion $g(t) = h(t) - 2\cosh(t/2)$ (NS101 Eq. (2): $F(z) = \tfrac{1}{4}(z^2 - \tfrac{1}{4})\int_{\mathbb{R}} g(t)e^{zt}\,dt$ for $|\operatorname{Re} z| < 1/2$), or (b) integrate by parts in $p$ across the slice integrals $A_p(y)$ to dominate the negative slices.
5. Remaining transfer & verdict Rejected (Fourier polarization tautology and three independent NS100/101 obstructions).
  • Polarization tautology: Because $\int_{\mathbb{R}} \mathcal{A}[f_x \pm b_x](t)e^{2iyt}\,dt = \tfrac{1}{2}|F(x+iy) \pm F'(x+iy)|^2$, the domination $\widehat{\mathcal{A}[f_x+b_x]}(2y) > \widehat{\mathcal{A}[f_x-b_x]}(2y)$ is algebraically identical to $|F + F'|^2 - |F - F'|^2 = 4\operatorname{Re}(F'\overline{F}) > 0$; it supplies no intermediate bound.
  • Slicewise positivity falsified (NS100 Eq. (2)): The 256/384-bit Arb certificate encloses $A_0(20) = \int_0^\infty \phi(t)^2\cos(40t)\,dt \in [-5.4320\times 10^{-10}, -5.4319\times 10^{-10}] < 0$, and dominated convergence forces $A_p(20) < 0$ for all sufficiently small $p > 0$.
  • General-property controls (NS100 & NS101 Eqs. (5)–(8)): Positivity, evenness, and strong log-concavity ($\partial_t^2 \log k \le -1/4$) fail on $k(t) = \frac{1}{2\sqrt{\pi}}\{3e^{-t^2/4}+e^{-(t-1)^2/4}+e^{-(t+1)^2/4}\}$ ($\operatorname{Re}(F_k'\overline{F_k})(\log 2 + i\pi) < 0$). Likewise, Jacobi reciprocity $\Theta_a(r) = r^{-1/2}\Theta_a(1/r)$, positive Gaussian weights, positive even kernel $\phi_a(t) > 0$, and order-one entire growth fail on the dilated theta control $\Theta_a(r) = [3\Theta(r) + e^{-a/2}\Theta(e^{-2a}r) + e^{a/2}\Theta(e^{2a}r)]/B_a$ at $a=2$, which has explicit off-axis critical-strip zeros $z = \pm\tfrac{1}{2}\operatorname{arcosh}(3/2) + i(m+\tfrac{1}{2})\pi \in \{|\operatorname{Re} z| < 1/2\}$.
  • Absolute unfolding boundary (NS101 Eqs. (9)–(11)): Termwise Mellin unfolding of $q_n(t)$ has $\int_{\mathbb{R}} |q_n(t)|e^{xt}\,dt = c(x)n^{-(x+1/2)}$, converging absolutely only for $x > 1/2$ outside the open critical strip, with an exact interchange failure at $z = 1/2$ ($\int_{\mathbb{R}} (\sum_{n\ge 1} q_n(t))e^{t/2}\,dt = 1/4 \ne 0 = \sum_{n\ge 1} \int_{\mathbb{R}} q_n(t)e^{t/2}\,dt$).

Candidate II — Tangent-Circle Power-Product Zero Statistic & Cayley Disk Contraction (NS94/100)

1. Exact expression For inversion circles of radii $p, q > 0$ externally tangent to the critical line $\operatorname{Re} s = 1/2$ at $1/2$ (centres $1/2 - p$ and $1/2 + q$), the nonnegative power-product sum over nontrivial zeros $\rho = x + iy$ up to height $H$ (NS94 Eqs. (89)–(91)): $$\mathcal{P}_{p,q}(H) := \sum_{\substack{\rho = x+iy \\ |y| \le H}} -\frac{P_L(I_L\rho)\,P_R(I_R\rho)}{p^3 q^3} = \sum_{\substack{\rho = x+iy \\ |y| \le H}} \frac{(x - 1/2)^2}{\bigl[(x - 1/2 + p)^2 + y^2\bigr]\bigl[(x - 1/2 - q)^2 + y^2\bigr]} \ge 0,$$ together with the Cayley logarithmic-derivative disk observable $1 - |S(z)|^2 = \frac{4\operatorname{Re} L(z)}{|L(z)+1|^2}$ ($L = F'/F$, $S = (L-1)/(L+1)$; NS100).
2. Coefficient hypotheses Functional equation $\xi(1-s) = \xi(s)$, conjugation symmetry $\overline{\xi(s)} = \xi(\bar{s})$, and Hadamard product convergence over symmetric zero sets.
3. Uniform range All finite height cutoffs $H \to \infty$ (requiring $\mathcal{P}_{p,q}(H) = 0$) or all $\operatorname{Re} z > 0$ (requiring $|S(z)| < 1$).
4. Estimating method Use separate or successive circle inversions $I_{c,r}(z) = c + r^2/(\bar{z}-c)$, the exterior homothety center $e = \tfrac{1}{2} + \frac{pq}{p-q}$, or inverted-disc zero-count comparisons to force $\mathcal{P}_{p,q}(H) = 0$.
5. Remaining transfer & verdict Rejected (zero-free coordinate dependence and NS94 symmetric quadruplet control).
  • Zero-independent geometry (NS94 Eqs. (30), (48)): Both the exterior homothety center $e = \tfrac{1}{2} + \frac{pq}{p-q}$ and the successive inversion Möbius map $I_R I_L(z) - \tfrac{1}{2} = \frac{z - 1/2}{1 + (p^{-1}+q^{-1})(z - 1/2)}$ depend exclusively on the circle radii $(p,q)$ and are completely independent of the zeros of $\xi$.
  • Symmetric off-line control (NS94 Eq. (75)): The real functional-equation polynomial $P(s) = ((s - \tfrac{1}{2} - \delta)^2 + t^2)((s - \tfrac{1}{2} + \delta)^2 + t^2)$ at $\delta = 1/4, t = 1$ satisfies $P(1-s) = P(s)$ and $\overline{P(s)} = P(\bar{s})$, places exactly two roots inside each image disc for $p = q = 1/2$ with zero roots on either boundary, and has the same homothety center at infinity, yet has $\mathcal{P}_{1/2,1/2} > 0$.
  • No arithmetic upper bound on $\mathcal{P}_{p,q}(\infty)$: While $\mathcal{P}_{p,q}(H) \ge 0$ avoids cancellation between reflected partners $\rho$ and $1-\bar{\rho}$, proving $\mathcal{P}_{p,q}(H) = 0$ for all $H$ is an unestimated RH-equivalent statement, not a consequence of inversion geometry or functional-equation symmetry.

Candidate III — Connected Contact Reeb Transport & Local Multi-Prime Operator Bridges (OPERATOR-BRIDGES: two-prime-contact & geometric-swarm)

1. Exact expression On the connected contact 3-manifold $Y = \{H=1\} \subset T^*(S^1 \times \mathbb{R})$ with $H = v(x)\tau + b(x)y$, $b(x) = \frac{x(1-x)}{x^2-x+1}$, $v(x) = \frac{a+c}{2} + \frac{a-c}{2}\cos(\pi x)$ ($a = 1/\log 2, c = 1/\log 3$), $\alpha = A\,dt + y\,dx$, and Reeb field $R = v\partial_t + b\partial_x - (v'A + b'y)\partial_y$, the damped horizontal-form transport $U_s = e^{-s/2}\varphi_{-s}^*$ on $\Omega_{\mathrm{hor},c}^\infty(Y)$ has local flat trace (two-prime-contact §2): $$\operatorname{Tr}^\flat(\chi U_s) = -\sum_{p \in \{2,3\}} \sum_{m \ge 1} (\log p)\,p^{-m/2}\,\delta(s - m\log p),$$ alongside the three local geometric-swarm constructions (hyperbolic $2n$-dimensional prime blocks, adelically metrized $\mathbb{P}^1 \times \mathbb{P}^1$ intersection pairings, and 4D oscillator / passive Euler ports).
2. Coefficient hypotheses Prescribed closed-orbit periods $T_p = \log p$ with transverse return eigenvalues $(p, p^{-1})$ and local unstable half-density weights $p^{-m/2}$.
3. Uniform range All primes $p \le e^{2a}$ across growing windows $a \to \infty$, realized on a completed Hilbert space that transfers to the full signed Weil quadratic form $q_a[f] \ge -C_*\|f\|_{L^2(-a,a)}^2$.
4. Estimating method Identify the Dyatlov–Zworski local flat trace of the Reeb transport (or local prime-block determinants) with a self-adjoint or contractive Hilbert-space operator whose spectral positivity implies the Weil floor.
5. Remaining transfer & verdict Rejected (arbitrary-period geometric control, purely continuous scalar $L^2$ spectrum, conserved-$\tau$ isolation vs. mixed-cycle determinant, and NS47 barrier).
  • Arbitrary-period control (two-prime-contact §§1, 5): Replacing $(\log 2, \log 3)$ by any distinct positive reals $(T_0, T_1)$ yields the exact same contact manifold, transverse return eigenvalues, and two-orbit local flat trace; primality is inserted as a parameter label, not derived or constrained by the geometry.
  • Wrong scalar $L^2$ spectrum (two-prime-contact §3): Under the exact straightening coordinates $u = \log|x/(1-x)| - x$, $\theta = t - F(x) \bmod 1$, $\tau = (1 - by)/v$, the contact form is $\alpha = du + \tau\,d\theta$ with volume $|\alpha \wedge d\alpha| = d\theta\,d\tau\,du$ and Reeb vector field $R = \partial_u$. The scalar Hilbert space $L^2(Y, |\alpha \wedge d\alpha|)$ is unitarily equivalent to $\bigoplus_{j=1}^3 L^2(S^1 \times \mathbb{R}_\tau \times \mathbb{R}_u)$, on which the Koopman generator $D = -i\partial_u$ has purely absolutely continuous spectrum $\mathbb{R}$ and zero eigenvalues.   • Isolation vs. mixed-orbit dichotomy (two-prime-contact §§2, 4): Conserved momentum $\tau$ prevents any full cotangent trajectory from connecting the $x=0$ ($\tau = \log 2$) and $x=1$ ($\tau = \log 3$) periodic orbits at both ends ($y = (1-\tau v(x))/b(x)$ blows up at one endpoint).   • Local-swarm companion gaps (geometric-swarm §1 & NS47): Independent passive prime ports fail contractivity (explicit counterexample $7/6 > 1$), elliptic prime-power deck maps collapse to identities, adelic valuations on $\mathbb{P}^1 \times \mathbb{P}^1$ lack a global test-to-metric identification with $q_a[f]$, and any single ordinary-$L^2$-closable positive square plus bounded remainder is barred by Theorem thm:ns47-single-square.
