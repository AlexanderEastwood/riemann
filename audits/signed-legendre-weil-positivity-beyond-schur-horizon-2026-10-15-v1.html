1. Theorem 1: Universal Indicator Completion of the Square Across u = 1 & All-Degree Reflection Strictness

For every shift τ ∈ (0, 2b) with normalized parameter u = τ/b ∈ (0, 2), the support multiplicity weight of Sτ + S−τ on (−b, b) is given globally across both the two-sided regime u ∈ (0, 1) and the single-reflection regime u ∈ [1, 2) by the sum of two indicator functions:

w_τ(t) = 1_{(−b, b−τ)}(t) + 1_{(−b+τ, b)}(t),    ⟨f, (D_τ + σ(S_τ + S_{−τ}))f⟩ = ∫_{−b}^{b−τ} (f(t) + σf(t+τ))2 dt ≥ 0.
  • Why Dτ(N) and Qτ,σ(N) Have Zero Piecewise Branching at u = 1: When u ∈ (0, 1), (−b, b−τ) and (−b+τ, b) overlap on (−b+τ, b−τ) to give weight {1, 2, 1}; when u ∈ [1, 2), they are disjoint and give weight {1, 0, 1}. Because the indicator sum is a single expression for all u ∈ (0, 2), completing the square inside ∫−bb−τ produces a single polynomial integral on [−1, 1−u] with zero branching at u = 1.
  • All-Degree Polynomial Kernel Proof: Since (−b, b−τ) has strictly positive length 2b − τ > 0 for all u ∈ (0, 2), a polynomial f satisfies ∫−bb−τ (f(t) + σf(t+τ))2 dt = 0 if and only if f(t+τ) = −σf(t) for all t ∈ ℝ. Since the only periodic/anti-periodic polynomials on ℝ are f ≡ 0 (for σ = +1) and f ≡ const ∈ span{e0} (for σ = −1), for every finite dimension N ≥ 1 and every u ∈ (0, 2): Qτ,+1(N,even) ≻ 0 on ℝN, Qτ,+1(N,odd) ≻ 0 on ℝN, Q̃τ,−1(N,even) ≻ 0 on ℝN−1 (with ker = ℝe0), and Qτ,−1(N,odd) ≻ 0 on ℝN.
  • Exact Signed-vs-Unsigned Legendre Decomposition: Wsigned(N,sector)(b) − Wunsigned(N,sector)(b) = ΔSchur(N,sector)(b) := ∑0<τr<2b |cr| Qτr,sgn(cr)(N,sector) ≻ 0.
