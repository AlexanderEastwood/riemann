What remains to estimate

The taper has an exact interior description. Set L=log N, SN=Σn≤N μ(n) log(N/n)/n, and Ψ1(t)=Σm≤t Λ(m) log(t/m), where Λ is the prime-power weight. For 1≤t≤N, its residual obeys

L RN(1/t) = SNt − Ψ1(t).

Thus we need control of the integrated squared discrepancy over a growing interval, as well as the remaining tail. Pointwise prime-counting asymptotics alone do not supply that estimate. This identifies the arithmetic issue but does not solve it.

The logarithmic taper is established prior art. Bettin–Conrey–Farmer’s theorem gives an optimal unsmoothed asymptotic under RH and an additional inverse-zeta-derivative moment hypothesis. We have used neither hypothesis as an unconditional input.
