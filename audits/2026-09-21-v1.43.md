## 4. Logic (question 3)

- **Derivative floor throughout the root interval:** yes, by archived gate,
  not by sampling. The eight v142 subintervals are interval evaluations of
  `hat f'` over each *whole* closed subinterval of length 1/40, minus the
  rigorous `E_1 = sqrt(L^3/12) sqrt(rho/b)`; they are contiguous and cover
  the closed interval `[gamma_1 - 1/10, gamma_1 + 1/10]`; minimum lower
  bound `0.00163 > 0.0016`. (N1 for traceability.)
- **First-positive-zero exclusion:** complete (N5).
- **Mean value step:** `z` and `gamma_1` both lie in the derivative
  interval (`z` by `prop:v142-ground-zero`), so
  `|z - gamma_1| <= |hat h(gamma_1)|/d < 1.400334e-35/0.0016 = 8.7521e-33`.
  Correct, and strict.
- **Lower-bound algebra:** with `||f|| = 1`, `h = P f`, `e = f - h`,
  spectral orthogonality gives `rho = mu_0 ||h||^2 + q[e]`, `q[e] >= b
  ||e||^2` (uses exactly the hypothesis "excited even spectrum `>= b`";
  `e` is even and orthogonal to the ground). Then `rho >= l(1 - ||e||^2)
  + b||e||^2 = l + (b - l)||e||^2` gives `eq:v143-lower-norm`;
  `q[e] = rho - mu_0||h||^2 <= Delta + l||e||^2 <= Delta + l q[e]/b`
  gives `eq:v143-lower-energy` (the displayed intermediate at line 8833
  is correct as written, with `l`, not `mu_0`, multiplying `q[e]/b`);
  the lemma applied to `e` gives `eq:v143-lower-transfer`; and
  `C b Delta/(b - rho + Delta) <= (d eps)^2` rearranges to
  `eq:v143-required-lower`. The second checker uses the other
  rearrangement (`check_discrepancy_budget.py:69-71`) and agrees.
- **"A lower bound alone does not reduce the direct bound":** correct as a
  statement about the formula `sqrt(C_ell rho)/d`, since a lower bound on
  `mu_0` gives no upper bound on `q[h] = mu_0 ||h||^2 <= rho`. See M2 for
  what it does not say.
- **Resolution comparison:** `R_0 / 2.9251e-71 = 2.99e38 > 1e38` and
  head-only `2.5174e-35 / 3e-71 = 8.4e35 > 1e35`, as stated. The text
  correctly calls this a failure of the displayed bounds, not of the
  object, and not a lower bound on the complete discrepancy.
