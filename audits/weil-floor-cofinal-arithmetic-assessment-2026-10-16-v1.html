Tightly Scoped Arithmetic-Lemma Assessment for the Cofinal Shifted Weil Floor (WEIL-FLOOR) and KERNEL-API

Date: October 16, 2026 · Version: v1 (companion to nb-full-residual-arithmetic-assessment-2026-10-16-v1.html and coeff-specialist-brief-2026-10-16-v18.html) · Decision Budget: ≤ 90 minutes pre-computation · Disposition: Stop report & continued pause (0 new computations)

1. Scope, Corrected Synthesis Baseline, and the Retained Sufficient Target

In coeff-specialist-brief-2026-10-16-v17.html (and v18), we corrected v16 by withdrawing its unestablished “Slepian ceiling” exclusion for the complete Weil operator: continuous Plancherel Fourier leakage above $\gamma_1$ in M16 v3 does not automatically bound the complete weighted discrete zero sum (plus polar terms). Removing that overstatement requires assessing the WEIL-FLOOR and KERNEL-API lanes directly against the actual sufficient cofinal target (prop:v121-cofinal-rh, prop:v136-bounded-floor, cor:v136-complement-floor, prop:v138-shifted-floor):

Sufficient Cofinal Shift-Floor Target (prop:v136-bounded-floor & cor:v136-complement-floor): For $a = \log\lambda > 0$ and $I_a = (-a,a)$, let $q_a[f]$ be the complete fixed-window Weil quadratic form on its logarithmic form domain $\mathcal{D}(q_a) = \{f \in L^2(I_a) : \int_{\mathbb{R}} \log(2+|\xi|)\,|\mathcal{F}_a f(\xi)|^2\,d\xi < \infty\}$, retaining the untruncated archimedean term, both pole terms $2\cosh((x-y)/2)$, and all prime-power shifts $w_n = \Lambda(n)/\sqrt{n}$ with $\ell_n = \log n < 2a$. To prove RH via the Weil criterion, it suffices to establish along one cofinal sequence $a_j \to \infty$ a single finite constant $C_* \ge 0$ independent of $j$ such that $$q_{a_j}[f] \ge -C_*\,\|f\|_{L^2(I_{a_j})}^2 \qquad \forall\, f \in \mathcal{D}(q_{a_j}),$$ or equivalently on $\mathcal{D}(q_{a_j}) \cap \operatorname{Ran}(I - P_{a_j})$ in both even and odd reflection sectors for any projection $P_a$ satisfying $\|\mathsf{W}_{e^a} P_a\| \to 0$ (such as the rank-$1$ repaired source projection or the growing fine-radical block $P_G$ of v1.53).

Structural Dichotomy with KERNEL-API (v1.56 / NS57): Neither a uniform positive spectral gap ($q_a[f] \ge c_0\|f\|^2 > 0$) nor exponentially accurate resolution of every small eigenvalue is required. Moreover, by Theorem thm:ns57-bounded-floor-stability (v1.56), the cofinal floor $q_a[f] \ge -C_*\|f\|^2$ is invariant under any fixed bounded self-adjoint perturbation $B$ (including any finite prime-weight modification $B_\delta = -\sum_{n \in S} \delta_n(T_{\ell_n} + T_{-\ell_n})$, which shifts $C_*$ by at most $2\sum_{n\in S}|\delta_n|$). By contrast, finite-window nullvector exclusion (KERNEL-API, thm:ns57-first-zero-controls) is broken by any nonzero finite perturbation $\delta \ne 0$, which creates a finite first-zero window $a_*(\delta) < \infty$.

Under the same discipline applied to the Nyman–Beurling lane in nb-full-residual-arithmetic-assessment-2026-10-16-v1.html, we enforce a hard pre-computation decision budget (≤ 90 minutes, zero new tables or adjacent scans), reject everything already covered by NS19–NS50, NS51–NS58, and M1–M17, and require all five mandatory items for each candidate mechanism: (1) exact expression, (2) coefficient hypotheses, (3) uniform range, (4) estimating method, and (5) remaining transfer.

2. Five-Item Assessment of Post-v18 Candidate Mechanisms

Candidate I — Shifted Block-Schur / Fourier-Tail Relative Form Comparison (prop:v138-shifted-floor + M17 v2)

1. Exact expression For a fixed finite shift $\delta = C_* \ge 0$, either the shifted Fourier Schur lower matrix (prop:v138-shifted-floor, Eq. eq:v138-shifted-lower) $$\mathcal{L}_\delta := K_\delta - \sum_{N < n \le J} \frac{R_{\delta,n}^* R_{\delta,n}}{g_n + \delta} - \frac{U_J}{g_{J+1}+\delta} \succeq 0 \implies \mathsf{W}_\lambda \succeq -\delta I,$$ or the spatial Legendre Block-Schur decomposition (M17 v2) on $L^2(-a,a) = V_K(a) \oplus V_K(a)^\perp$: $$(\mathsf{W}_{e^a} + C_* I)\big|_{V_K(a)^\perp} = P_{V_K}^\perp\bigl(A_{\mathrm{arch}}^{\mathrm{full}}(a) + T_{\mathrm{pole}}(a) - T_{\mathrm{arith}}(a) + C_* I\bigr)P_{V_K}^\perp.$$
2. Coefficient hypotheses Exact von Mangoldt prime-power weights $w_n = \Lambda(n)/\sqrt{n}$ for $\ell_n = \log n < 2a$, untruncated archimedean digamma symbol $\alpha(\xi) = \operatorname{Re}\psi(\tfrac{1}{4}+\tfrac{i\xi}{2}) - \log\pi$, and exact polar rank-two kernel $2\cosh((x-y)/2)$.
3. Uniform range Cofinal windows $a_j \to \infty$ ($\lambda_j = e^{a_j} \to \infty$) in both even and odd reflection sectors with a single window-independent shift $\delta = C_* < \infty$ and tractable head dimension $N(a), K(a) \le a^{O(1)}$.
4. Estimating method Replace the scalar tail comparison $g_{N+1} + \delta > 0$ (which uses only the triangle-inequality envelope $M_{\phi,\lambda} \ge \|\mathsf{T}_{\mathrm{pr},\lambda}\| \asymp \lambda/\log\lambda$) by a signed relative form bound on the high-degree complement $y \in V_K(a)^\perp \cap \mathcal{D}(q_a)$: $$\bigl|\langle T_{\mathrm{arith}}(a) y, y\rangle_{L^2(-a,a)}\bigr| \le \theta\,\langle A_{\mathrm{arch}}^{\mathrm{full}}(a) y, y\rangle_{L^2(-a,a)} + C_*\,\|y\|_{L^2(-a,a)}^2$$ with $\theta \le 1$ and $C_* < \infty$ independent of $a$ for $K(a) \le a^{O(1)}$.
5. Remaining transfer & verdict Rejected (no estimating lemma for the relative form bound). In prop:v138-shifted-floor (Eq. eq:v138-shifted-cost), any scalar comparison with bounded $\delta = C_*$ forces $N+1 > 2a\exp(M_{\phi,\lambda} - \delta) = \exp(\Omega(e^a/a))$; similarly, in M17 v2, bounding $T_{\mathrm{arith}}(a)$ on $V_K(a)^\perp$ by its operator norm $\|T_{\mathrm{arith}}(a)\| \asymp e^a/a$ against the harmonic floor $\inf_{V_K(a)^\perp} A_{\mathrm{arch}}^{\mathrm{full}}(a) = \log K - \log a + O(1)$ forces $K(a) \ge a\exp(\Omega(e^a/a))$. Proving the non-scalar relative form bound on $V_K(a)^\perp$ for $\theta = 1$ is equivalent to bounding $\int_{\mathbb{R}} \beta_a(\xi)\,|\mathcal{F}_a y(\xi)|^2\,d\xi \ge -C_*\|y\|_2^2$ on $V_K(a)^\perp$. By Corollary cor:v137-scalar-no-go and Theorem thm:v139-probe-relaxation, neither pointwise bounds on $\beta_a(\xi)$ nor scalar primitive / total-variation bounds can supply this inequality, because $\inf_\xi \beta_a(\xi) \to -\infty$ and $a\Delta(\beta_a) \to +\infty$. Neither M13–M14 (exact Legendre shift matrices) nor M17 supplies an oscillation mechanism that cancels the negative spikes of $\beta_a(\xi)$ near critical zeros on $V_K(a)^\perp$.

Candidate II — Fine Translated-Radical Block Complement & Paley–Wiener Zero-Neighborhood Exclusion (v1.53 / NS46/49 + v1.39)

1. Exact expression In v1.53 (Corollary ns46-a2-cor-large-block), let $J_a = \lfloor e^{2a}/(40000a)\rfloor$, $m_a = 2J_a+1$, $t_j = j/J_a \in [-1,1]$, and $g_{a,t_j} = \chi_a \phi(\cdot - t_j)$, where $\phi = h/\|h\|_2$ is the normalized even Gaussian arithmetic radical ($\mathcal{W}\phi_t = 0$). The orthogonal projection $P_G = G\Gamma^{-1}G^*$ onto $\operatorname{Ran} G \subset L^2(-a,a)$ satisfies $\|\mathsf{W}_{e^a} P_G\| \le C\exp(-e^{2a}/2000) \to 0$. By Corollary cor:v136-complement-floor, the full cofinal floor reduces exactly to $$q_a[y] = \int_{\mathbb{R}} \beta_a(\xi)\,|\mathcal{F}_a y(\xi)|^2\,d\xi \ge -C_*\,\|y\|_2^2 \qquad \forall\, y \in \mathcal{D}(q_a) \cap \operatorname{Ran}(I - P_G).$$
2. Coefficient hypotheses Exact global radical identity $\mathcal{W}\phi_t = 0$, Mellin/Fourier identification $\widehat{\phi}(\xi) = \frac{2}{\sqrt{6\pi}\|h\|_2}\Xi(\xi)$ (Eq. eq:v150-mellin), double-exponential spatial tail $\|\mathsf{W}_{e^a} g_{a,t_j}\|_2 \le C_1\sqrt{a}\,e^{2a}e^{-c_1 e^{2a}}$, and finite-arc Lagrange interpolation Gram bound $\Gamma \succeq \frac{1}{2}\gamma_{m_a} I$ (Lemma lem:ns46-finite-arc).
3. Uniform range All unit vectors $y \in \mathcal{D}(q_a) \cap \operatorname{Ran}(I - P_G)$ in both reflection sectors along a cofinal sequence $a_j \to \infty$, with one finite $C_*$ independent of $j$.
4. Estimating method Exploit the $m_a = 2J_a+1 \asymp e^{2a}/a$ orthogonality constraints $\langle y, \chi_a \phi(\cdot - t_j)\rangle = 0$ ($-J_a \le j \le J_a$) in Fourier space: $$\int_{\mathbb{R}} \mathcal{F}_a y(\xi)\,\overline{\widehat{\phi}(\xi)}\,e^{-it_j\xi}\,d\xi = -\langle y, (1-\chi_a)\phi(\cdot - t_j)\rangle = O\bigl(e^{-c_1 e^{2a}}\bigr),$$ and attempt to use fine-lattice sampling on $t_j \in [-1,1]$ to prevent $|\mathcal{F}_a y(\xi)|^2$ from concentrating in the negative sublevel sets of $\beta_a(\xi)$ near critical-zero ordinates $\gamma_0$.
5. Remaining transfer & verdict Rejected (vanishing of $\widehat{\phi}(\gamma_0) \propto \Xi(\gamma_0) = 0$ screens critical-zero wavepackets from $P_G$). By Proposition prop:v137-conditional-mass and Theorem thm:v139-probe-relaxation, the negative spikes of $\beta_a(\xi)$ that obstruct a bounded floor occur inside $O(1/a)$ neighborhoods of nontrivial zero ordinates $\gamma_0$ of $\Xi(\xi)$, where $\beta_a(\xi)$ has negative side lobes of depth $\asymp -a$. However, at every such ordinate $\gamma_0$, the radical multiplier $\widehat{\phi}(\xi) = \frac{2}{\sqrt{6\pi}\|h\|_2}\Xi(\xi)$ vanishes ($\widehat{\phi}(\gamma_0) = 0$, so $|\widehat{\phi}(\xi)| = O(|\xi - \gamma_0|) = O(1/a)$ on $|\xi - \gamma_0| \le 3\pi/(2a)$). Consequently, the sampling functional $\xi \mapsto \mathcal{F}_a y(\xi)\,\overline{\widehat{\phi}(\xi)}$ is automatically suppressed by $O(1/a)$ near every zero $\gamma_0$. As already recorded in v1.53 (lines 17946–17962), the fine-lattice Gram bound in Lemma lem:ns46-finite-arc controls $P_G$ via $|\widehat{\phi}(\xi)|^2 \ge c_0 > 0$ on the zero-free interval $[-r,r]$, and supplies no lower bound on $\operatorname{Ran}(I - P_G)$ near the zeros of $\Xi$.

Candidate III — Multiprime Euler Preconditioning & Boundary-Pairing Depletion (M6b + M7–M10 + NS57/58)

1. Exact expression For a finite prime set $S \subset \mathbb{P}$ with shift sum $L_S = \sum_{p \in S} \log p < a$, either the multiprime Euler preconditioner $E_S(-i\partial_x) = \prod_{p \in S}(I - p^{-1/2}T_{\log p}) : L^2(I_{a-L_S}) \to L^2(I_a)$ (M7–M13, M6b) or the translated-radical boundary pairing $\mathcal{B}_a f(t) = \langle f, R_{a,t}\rangle = -q_a(f, u_t)$ of Proposition prop:ns58-boundary-identity (NS58).
2. Coefficient hypotheses Exact multiplicative Euler product factors $\prod_{p \in S}(1 - p^{-1/2-i\xi})^{-1}$ versus additive von Mangoldt shifts $w_n = \Lambda(n)/\sqrt{n}$, or exact exterior radical row $R_{a,t}$ with infinite prime sum $\sum_{n \ge 2} w_n\{r_t(x-\ell_n)+r_t(x+\ell_n)\}$ retained.
3. Uniform range Full space $L^2(-a,a)$ across cofinal windows $a \to \infty$ (for WEIL-FLOOR) or arbitrary fixed window $a > 0$ (for KERNEL-API).
4. Estimating method Cancel the dominant small-prime shifts $p \in S$ via the congruence $E_S^* \mathsf{W}_{e^a} E_S$ on $L^2(I_{a-L_S})$, or test $f \in \ker A_a$ against the exterior radical rows $R_{a,t}$ across $t \in \mathbb{R}$ to force $f = 0$.
5. Remaining transfer & verdict Rejected (infinite codimension of $\operatorname{Ran} E_S \subset L^2(I_a)$, bounded-floor redundancy of finite $S$, and NS58 kernel equivalence).
  • For WEIL-FLOOR ($q_a[f] \ge -C_*\|f\|^2$ as $a \to \infty$): By Theorem thm:ns57-bounded-floor-stability, any finite set of prime-power shifts up to a fixed cutoff already forms a bounded operator on $L^2(\mathbb{R})$, so depleting finitely many primes $S$ cannot resolve the cofinal $a \to \infty$ divergence problem. Moreover, $E_S(L^2(I_{a-L_S})) \subsetneq L^2(I_a)$ has infinite codimension (missing the boundary fiber coordinates on $(-a, a) \setminus (-a+L_S, a-L_S)$), and if $S(a)$ grows with $a$, the constraint $\sum_{p \in S(a)} \log p < a$ forces $\max S(a) \lesssim a$, leaving all prime shifts in $(a, e^{2a}]$ uncancelled.
  • For KERNEL-API ($f \in \ker A_a \implies f = 0$): Proposition prop:ns58-equivalence (NS58) already proves that vanishing of the boundary pairing $\mathcal{B}_a f(t) = 0$ for all $t \in \mathbb{R}$ is an exact reformulation of $A_a f = 0$, while Proposition prop:ns58-forced-pairing proves that the NS57 forcing operator $F_\delta$ is Hilbert–Schmidt (compact) and therefore has no positive $L^2$ lower bound on the unit sphere of $L^2(I_a)$.
