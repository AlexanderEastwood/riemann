### 3. ZLD, QG and CAE: the arrows, with their quantifiers

**Exact identity and equivalence.** Write `Z_a = beta_a` for the *complete corrected* zero field and `g=f/h`. The identity at `T:11602–11612` is

`q_a[f] = integral Z_a |F f|^2 = J_h[g] - (2/3) Var_nu(g) - 2 |<f,sinh(x/2)>|^2`.

For the same specified C and the same complete domain, CAE is therefore **equivalent**, in both directions, to the floor `q_a[f] >= -C ||f||^2` on every even `f perpendicular u_a` and every odd f. It is not merely a sufficient bound for that complement. Cofinal CAE requires a single C independent of a. At one window `C=D_a` is automatic and contains no uniform conclusion (`T:11630–11643`). Division by h is justified locally; no uniform bound on that multiplication operator is being smuggled in (`T:11674–11678`).

Complement CAE gives a full floor only after the source residual and cross-term lemma are invoked: `T:6136–6156`, `T:12700–12725`. A full floor restricts to the complement, but the constants need not match in the reverse transfer. For this established arithmetic family, RH gives the complement floor with C=0 by Weil positivity; conversely uniform complement CAE plus the established source residual implies RH by v1.36. Thus it is RH-strength here, not an easier sign estimate supplied by a coordinate change.

**Proved implication, one fixed family and head rule.** Fix the same cofinal family, prescribed integer N(a), `X_a=2 pi N(a)/L`, and the same *global* depth D_a. Uniform ZLD means the normalized negative-level measure dominates `(kappa/D_a) dt` on `[-theta D_a,0]`, with kappa, theta bounded below by positive constants. Its quantization consequence is

`QC_K(m_a) >= kappa theta^2 D_a / (2K)`.

Together with the unconditional `D_a -> infinity`, this proves the **growth clause** of QG for every fixed K. It does not give the separate packet clause: exact source admissibility, unit norm, all-Borel domination `mu_g >= c0 m_a`, and original energy `q_a[g] <= Q_*` uniformly (`T:11177–11191`, `T:11614–11628`). Finite-bin feasibility, including the recent odd diagnostics, is not all-Borel domination. ZLD alone is not full QG as that assumption is packaged.

**Proved implication to a failed minorant bound, not to negativity of q.** QG growth **plus** those packets gives, for the specified step-minorant class,

`eta(m_a) >= [c0 QC_K(m_a) - Q_*]_+`.

Consequently bounded K forces the best ordinary lower error of that minorant to diverge; bounded error forces `K(a) -> infinity` by applying the argument to any bounded-K subsequence (`T:11043–11080`, `T:11193–11198`). It does **not** imply a linear rate in D. That needs ZLD's quantitative estimate or additional quantization-shape control (`T:11203–11214`). K counts distinct values, not the number of overlapping indicator sets (`T:10458–10460`). No converse “level-count obstruction implies QG” is proved; other obstructions could cause the same conclusion.

There is no established arrow from ZLD or QG to CAE. Their purpose in this chain is to obstruct low-complexity sufficient certificates, not to prove positivity. Nor is CAE shown to imply either for a specified natural head rule. The general level-measure countermodels refute universal measure-theoretic converses; they do not establish logical independence for zeta. The diluting-head construction refutes a claim uniform over *all* head rules, without deciding a previously prescribed natural rule (`T:11700–11741`). Changing N(a) midway changes the assertion.

**Assessment of v1.51:** the comparison is complete for these immediate identities, implications and limitations. It explicitly does not settle all relations between the actual arithmetic conjectures. Its wording is sound. The proposed summary “three equivalent-looking open inputs about the zeros” is unsound: CAE is the signed target, while the other two are scalar distribution/complexity inputs with additional packet obligations.

**What would make an input easier?** An independently proved arithmetic distribution theorem on the declared rule could establish ZLD or QG without proving positivity; that would give a new complexity obstruction, not RH. A proof reducing them to a stated, separately tractable theorem with uniform remainders would be real progress of that kind. CAE would become useful only through a new estimate of its full signed energy, with poles, exterior edges, source condition and mixtures retained. The identity itself has made none of these inputs easier by proof. No available theorem shows ZLD or QG logically weaker or stronger than RH for the actual field.

## Part B — untried routes and the first tests

**No new validated RH route is claimed.** Three candidate directions are assessed below. The first fails as a reduction to ordinary pair statistics; the second leaves a concrete, unexcluded kernel question; the third leaves a nonlinear deformation problem rather than the proposed linear bridge. None is promoted because an obstruction happens not to cover it. The new questions are not more windows, narrower enclosures, unsigned norms, or another tail metric.

### B1. Full-zero statistics rather than ordinary pair correlation

**Proposed statement — open input, not a known conjecture.** On a declared cofinal family, prove a uniform lower bound for the complete corrected zero field paired against every physical density:

`integral Z_a(xi) |F_a f(xi)|^2 dxi >= -C ||f||^2`,

with the complete source/parity conditions if using a complement. An actual statistical route must identify a separate statement S about the full zeros, with real parts and multiplicities, and prove `S => this inequality` with endpoint and Perron errors uniform in a. Merely naming that inequality “repulsion” changes nothing.

**First falsifiable step — assessed and fails for ordinary limiting pair statistics.** Perturb a model zero set by adding one fixed off-line quartet while retaining symmetry. Such a finite perturbation is invisible to leading, normalized high-height pair-correlation limits, but its terms in v1.46 carry factors `exp(L (Re rho-1/2))`. Those are not bounded by ordinate spacings at scale 1/L. Likewise density-one conclusions allow exceptional zeros; CAE would exclude every off-line zero. This is a test of the information retained by the proposed statistic, not construction of another zeta function or a proof of arithmetic logical independence. The scales also differ: Montgomery statistics normally normalize by mean spacing near height T, whereas 1/L here is imposed by the physical support and the moving resolvent.

Original Montgomery results use RH. The unconditional extension of Baluyot–Goldston–Suriajaya–Turnage-Butterbaugh supplies a particular quadratic average, and the Goldston–Lee–Schettler–Suriajaya result obtains a density-one conclusion from a formulation of PCC. Neither stated conclusion is an all-height bound for this signed field. The existing NS-40 assessment already identifies moving-parameter, cutoff and level-mass gaps, so another raw pair-correlation calculation would duplicate explored work. [BGSTB](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.04799), [Goldston et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.15449).

**Why the known obstructions are not the issue.** A theorem retaining every weighted zero and the physical Fourier range would not admit the artificial v1.39 probe or arbitrary protected rotations; it need not use v1.25's far majorant. The problem is missing horizontal, exceptional-zero, remainder and operator-test control. If S really implied CAE without additional RH-strength hypotheses, S would imply RH; calling it strictly weaker than RH would then be unjustified. No such known or conjectured *weaker-than-RH* S was established in the sources inspected. This candidate does not survive as an ordinary spacing shortcut.

### B2. Continue the sign by excluding a zero crossing, rather than estimating a positive gap

**What has to change.** Nested physical supports give a **nonincreasing** lower edge, `mu(b) <= mu(a)` for b>a. Thus a known lower bound at a smaller window gives no lower bound at larger windows. Interlacing and the nesting of Sonine spaces must not be conflated without an operator/form intertwining. This elementary direction test already defeats the proposed monotonicity shortcut (`T:6026–6045`).

An exact growth condition that would work is, on an unbounded sequence `a_j`,

`mu(a_(j+1)) >= mu(a_j) - r_j`, with `r_j >= 0` and `sum_j r_j < infinity`.

Then `mu(a_j) >= mu(a_0) - sum_j r_j` is a uniform floor, and v1.36 applies. In a continuous formulation one needs domination of the **entire downward-variation measure** by a finite measure; an integrable bound on an a.e. derivative alone is insufficient without absolute continuity. A bound such as `-C log a`, `-C a^epsilon`, or arbitrarily slow divergence does not suffice using the recorded theorem: its negative-test amplification gives no effective comparison rate (`T:12691–12700`). This is exact threshold accounting, not a new growth estimate. The scalar cutoff proposition does not forbid proving it analytically; using that same scalar majorant to calculate it retains its cost.

**Distinct structural candidate — open input.** Prove `ker(A_a)={0}` for every `a >= log 4` on the complete operator domain. Continuity of the lowest eigenvalue and the positive lambda=4 ground would then forbid a first crossing of zero. A compact-resolvent lower-bounded operator attains its lowest eigenvalue, so a zero crossing produces an actual kernel. This implication needs no uniform positive gap and is compatible with positive eigenvalues approaching zero. It is a published nondegeneracy formulation, not a new RH equivalence. [Suzuki, Theorem 1.3 and its following discussion](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.09096v1#S1.SS2); [CCM, Corollaries 3.7–3.8](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.22755v1#S3).

`A_a f=0 => f in H_0^1(-a,a) and G_a Df=0`,

where Suzuki's symmetric core realization is `B_a=D* G_a D` and A_a is its Friedrichs extension. For f already in `H_0^1`, the adjoint equation `D* G_a Df=0` makes `G_a Df` constant, and its zero-mean range makes that constant zero. The first, unproved regularity implication is the test. The repository's endpoint theorem proves only boundedness, continuity and logarithmic boundary decay (`T:5272–5349`); it does not establish H1 regularity. ### B3. Reopen the heat-deformation bridge as a nonlinear identity problem

**What has to change.** v1.46 gives the exact zero expansion for the undeformed zeta symbol. It does not identify a deformed H_t symbol with a Gaussian smoothing of beta_a. For the conventional `H_t(z)=integral exp(tu^2) Phi(u) cos(zu) du`, direct differentiation gives the exact identity `partial_t H_t = -partial_z^2 H_t`. Away from its zeros, `L_t=partial_z log H_t` therefore satisfies

`partial_t L_t = -partial_z^2 L_t - 2 L_t partial_z L_t`.

The quadratic term is present. A proposed *linear* heat transport of the logarithmic-derivative/Perron field fails this first algebraic test. This is an identity derived here from the defining integral, not an estimated sign or an assumption about the zeros. [Rodgers–Tao](https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.05914), [Polymath15](https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.12438).

**Proposed replacement — open input.** Construct an exact family of sesquilinear zero forms q_(a,t), with the normalization at t=0 equal to the existing q_a, and prove a full signed comparison

`q_(a,0)[f] >= q_(a,t0)[f] - C ||f||^2`

with one C independent of a and a fixed time t0 where the deformed zeros are all real. Every support/domain, zero-sum convergence, multiplicity, normalization and pole correction has to be supplied. Real zeros give a sum-of-squares form only after this construction is justified. A time-derivative domination with an integrable uniform error would be one sufficient mechanism. It is currently absent and is not provided by v1.46 or by the known positive-time barrier.

**First falsifiable step.** Besides the failed linear identity above, compute the exact contribution and time derivative of a symmetry-paired two-zero collision before taking absolute values. Individual zero velocities have inverse-gap singularities; only the full paired expression can determine whether those singularities cancel in the proposed form and whether a remainder depends on a. A proposed transfer based on separately bounded velocities fails if it retains such a nonintegrable loss. This finite algebra exercise needs no new Weil window and can disqualify a candidate transfer formula within a day; it cannot establish the uniform comparison itself.

**Why the known obstructions do not settle it.** A complete nonlinear deformation argument retaining the theta/zero arithmetic and all signed cross terms is not a v1.39 relaxation and need not use v1.25's scalar cutoff. Conversely, replacing it by unsigned velocity sums or smoothed symbols would reinstate precisely the losses this project warns against. v1.46 removes no domain or uniformity gap for t>0. The correct status is “no bridge constructed,” not “bridge impossible.” No surviving numerical experiment here closes that gap.

### The requested larger admissible class

**Exact linear-algebra check.** In the physical Hilbert space the full source complement has codimension one. Any linear subspace properly containing it is the entire space; in the even sector the same statement holds relative to the even space. Requiring positivity on that larger class cannot, by inclusion alone, be an easier assertion. A convenient representation might make its *proof* easier, but no such representation with a new sign estimate was found here.

If “larger” meant removing more directions, that actually makes the tested complement **smaller**. A v1.36 analogue then needs an orthogonal projection into the complete operator domain, a uniform operator residual bound for the whole removed block, and the controlled cross-term transfer of `prop:v118-gap-free`; testing each removed vector individually does not bound the block operator norm. A uniformly bounded residual plus a uniform complement floor already gives a full uniform floor; a residual tending to zero is the stronger recorded version. The rank may grow, but no sign estimate disappears (`T:6136–6214`). This is already represented by the radical-block lane, including separate in-flight work, and is not proposed as a new route.

A form-core restriction could also suffice if every compact smooth test were approximable with convergence of both norm and form, including mixtures. Ordinary L2 density alone cannot transport an indefinite unbounded form. Finally, a parity-only RH criterion is published and was already noted in the 2026-09-21 audit, NOTE N2 and §7; it is not an unexplored discovery here. None of these changes turns finitely many sample vectors into a sufficient admissible class.

**Part B disposition:** ordinary pair correlation fails the information test; monotonicity points the wrong way unless supplemented by a genuinely uniform or summable signed estimate; enlarging the source complement supplies no relaxation; and the naive heat bridge fails its generator identity. Kernel exclusion and a fully nonlinear heat comparison remain unexcluded questions, with explicit first tests, but neither has a new arithmetic lemma establishing the necessary sign.
