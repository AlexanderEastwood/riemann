## 2. Four Main Theorems of Milestone 17

### Theorem 1 (Exact Two-Shift Operator Product & Finite-Rank Multi-Shift Coupling Gram Matrix)
For any $0 \le \tau_1 \le \tau_2 < 2b$ ($u_1 = \tau_1/b, u_2 = \tau_2/b \in [0, 2)$), on $L^2(-b, b)$ the product of two compressed symmetric shift operators satisfies the **exact operator identity**:
$$\bigl(P_b S_{\tau_1} P_b\bigr)\bigl(P_b S_{\tau_2} P_b\bigr) = P_b S_{\tau_1 + \tau_2} P_b + P_b S_{\tau_2 - \tau_1} P_b - D_{\tau_1, \tau_2}(b),$$
where for $m + n$ even, $\bigl(D_{\tau_1, \tau_2}(b)\bigr)_{m, n} = \sqrt{(2m+1)(2n+1)} \int_{\max(-1 + \Delta u,\, 1 - u_1)}^1 P_m(x)\,P_n(x - \Delta u)\,dx$ ($\Delta u = u_2 - u_1$). Consequently, for the combined shift operator $W_{\mathrm{comb}, R}^{\mathrm{off}}(b) = \sum_{0 < \tau_r < 2b} d_r(R)\,S_{\tau_r}^{(b)}$, the **infinite-dimensional low-to-high block-coupling Gram matrix** $\mathcal{G}_{\mathrm{comb}, R}^{(N_{\mathrm{leg}}, \mathrm{sector})}(b) = B_{\mathrm{comb}, R}^* B_{\mathrm{comb}, R}$ is an **exact $N_{\mathrm{leg}} \times N_{\mathrm{leg}}$ matrix** computed in closed form with **zero tail truncation error** and **100% preservation of signed cancellations**.
