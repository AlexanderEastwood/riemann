MAJOR M7 — The blanket six-relative-digit promise is not established

evidence/diag_true_symbol/results.md:196 · evidence/diag_true_symbol/pencil.py:4 · evidence/diag_pencil_arithmetic/results.md:57 · evidence/diag_lobe_mass/results.md:252

The correct perturbation argument is favorable but conditional. Write B=W+W⁻, normalize v*Bv=1, and hold W fixed. For a simple eigenvalue, differentiating Wv=νBv gives δν=−ν v*(δW⁻)v. Thus δν/ν=−v*(δW⁻)v. If −εW⁻≤δW⁻≤εW⁻ as quadratic forms and W≥0, then |δν|/|ν|≤ε(1−ν). More generally the relative error is controlled by ‖B−1/2δW⁻B−1/2‖. A small ν, including 10−100, does not itself amplify a denominator perturbation by 1/ν.

But entry spot checks of the signed full-symbol quadrature do not bound that denominator norm, and the tail script checks only two entries over a finite range. Its replay finds a λ=8 W⁻[0,1] omitted-tail contribution 2.85×10−6, or 3.3×10−4 relative; W⁻[0,0] is missing 4.2×10−5 relative. These do not prove a comparable error in each deep Rayleigh denominator—cancellation can make its error smaller—but they defeat the stated uniform 10−6 matrix-accuracy premise. For a normwise counterexample, W=diag(10−112,1), W⁻=diag(10−12,1), δW⁻=diag(10−6,0) has a 10−6 relative operator perturbation of W⁻ yet changes its tiny generalized eigenvalue from about 10−100 to 10−106. Relative form control is the missing qualification.

Finally W is not numerically exact in the solve: tomp drops the Arb radius and serializes dps−5 digits. At 120 digits the λ=6, N=96 ν₀ is −5.238×10−116; at 180 digits it is +6.58024×10−135. The “however small” guarantee is false below that arithmetic floor. The floor explanation and the 10−30 counts do survive the independent replay, documented below.
