Use the fixed q=2 space H=L²((0,∞), dt/t²), with target τ(t)=1t≥1 log t and integer atoms

2. What is controlled, and what is missing

NS87 represents the correlations by finite signed arithmetic observations. For a physical cutoff T, put PT(t)=∫tT log(u/t)rN(u) du/u² and

qn(T)=(1/n)∫0TPT(t)dt − ∑1≤k≤T/nPT(kn).

Write GN for the complete old Gram and CN for its old/new cross block. The corrected observation vector is

ĥN,T=qnew(T)−CNTGN−1qold(T).

Truncation destroys old-space orthogonality, so qold cannot be discarded. The residual itself also contains the optimized arithmetic coefficients. This is the first unestimated signed vector.

At T=N⁶, define 𝒜N=ĥN,TTHN−1ĥN,T. The existing complete-tail bound gives

|𝒜N/EN−ΔN/EN| = O(N−1/2 log²N).

This error is summable on N=2j. Divergence of the sum of normalized observations is therefore equivalent to convergence of the actual errors to zero, through the exact error product. It remains an RH-equivalent target, not a weaker intermediate lemma. The familiar positive lower limit of j𝒜2j/E2j is one stronger sufficient condition. Neither arithmetic assertion is proved here. The asymptotic tail rate has no effective onset in the inherited argument. This is bookkeeping from existing bounds, not a new convergence theorem.
