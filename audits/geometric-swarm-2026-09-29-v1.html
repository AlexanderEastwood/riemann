Three geometric construction briefs

Contact geometry in 4D, 6D and 8D. An explicit four-dimensional block has a central orbit of period log p. Its signed local trace coefficient matches the prime-power weight. Higher dimensions require adjusted expansion rates and grading to retain that coefficient. Composite labels pass the same algebra, so this alone does not identify the primes. A connected global construction, full trace theorem, gamma and pole terms, and positivity transfer are absent. Definitions and review.

Arithmetic surfaces. Products of elliptic curves give four real dimensions, but the proposed prime-power deck maps become identities after quotienting. A separate family of adelically metrized line bundles on P¹×P¹ retains prime valuations in its local metrics. The missing step is a complete map from the actual Weil tests to these metrics with the required controlled remainder. A Hodge sign theorem does not supply that map. Definitions and sources.

Spectral geometry. A four-dimensional oscillator produces a two-prime partition function; its energy levels are logarithms of integers, not zeta zeros. A completed canonical-system construction belongs to an existing program. The simplest independent passive-prime version fails: one factor has modulus 7/6 where contractivity would require at most 1. A coupled completion is still undefined. Construction limits.

Why a larger swarm would currently waste effort

All three local-model proposals reused the same supplied contact-block example despite different assignments. They confused geometric dimensions, local orbit weights, spectral eigenvalues and the global positivity target. The critics caught some defects but introduced others. For example, infinite measure does not prevent a spectral measure, and an explicitly conditional argument is not automatically circular. The second model often repeated the first critic’s mistakes.

We strengthened the lane-specific briefs and required critics to identify the exact assertion they dispute. Those revised prompts have not yet been tested in another inference batch. The audit of all nine replies records the corrections. Model agreement will not count as proof or automatic candidate admission.

Where I would work next

I would spend the next round defining one complete construction bridge, then use the local models to challenge its individual obligations. In the contact lane, the concrete assembly question is how to join the prime blocks without an uncancelled mixed primitive contribution at log 6. In the metric lane, it is how the proposed intersection pairing reproduces the complete signed Weil form. These questions must be made precise before another RH-directed scan.

A successful local construction test would narrow its own assembly problem. It would not establish the remaining global trace or arithmetic estimate. The existing freezes therefore remain in place.
