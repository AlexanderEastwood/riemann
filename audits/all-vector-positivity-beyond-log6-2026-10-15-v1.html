3. Key Mathematical Mechanisms

3.1 Sum-of-Squares Monotonicity on $(\tfrac{1}{2}\log 6, \log(5/2)]$

For $a \in (\tfrac{1}{2}\log 6, \log(5/2)]$, $b = a - \log 2 \le \tau_5 = \log(5/4)$, so on $L^2(0, b)$ the preconditioner is $P_0(a) = v_0\mathbf{1}_{[0, x_5]} + v_1\mathbf{1}_{[x_5, x_3]} + v_2\mathbf{1}_{[x_3, x_6]} + (v_2 I \pm c_6 R_{\tau_6})\mathbf{1}_{[x_6, b]}$ where $x_m = \tau_m - b$. Using $(v_2 I \pm c_6 R_{\tau_6})^{-1} = (v_2 I \mp c_6 R_{\tau_6})/(v_2^2 - c_6^2)$ and $\tfrac{db}{da} = 1, \tfrac{dx_m}{da} = -1$, differentiating $\langle P_0(a)^{-1} u_\xi, u_\xi \rangle_{L^2(0,b)}$ yields:

$\dfrac{\partial}{\partial a}\left\langle P_0(a)^{-1} u_\xi, u_\xi \right\rangle_{L^2(0,b)} = \left(\dfrac{1}{v_1} - \dfrac{1}{v_0}\right)u_\xi(x_5)^2 + \left(\dfrac{1}{v_2} - \dfrac{1}{v_1}\right)u_\xi(x_3)^2 + \dfrac{\bigl(v_2 u_\xi(b) \mp c_6 u_\xi(x_6)\bigr)^2}{v_2(v_2^2 - c_6^2)} \ge 0.$

Because $v_0 > v_1 > v_2 > c_6 > 0$ at $\Delta_\alpha = 0.35$, every term is non-negative for all $\xi \ge 0$, proving that $\mathcal{T}_{\mathrm{even/odd}}^{(0.35)}(a) \le \mathcal{T}_{\mathrm{even/odd}}^{(0.35)}(\log(5/2)) < 1$ across the entire interval $a \in (\tfrac{1}{2}\log 6, \log(5/2)]$.

3.2 $3\times 3$ Tridiagonal Fiber Bundle & 4-Step Midpoint-Moment Enclosure up to $\tfrac{1}{2}\log 7$

For $a \in (\log(5/2), \tfrac{1}{2}\log 7]$, $d_5 = b - \tau_5 \in (0, \tfrac{1}{2}\log(28/25)] \approx (0, 0.056664]$. The three intervals $I_1 = [0, d_5]$, $I_2 = [\tau_5, b]$, and $I_3 = [\tau_6 - b, \tau_6 - \tau_5]$ are pairwise disjoint of equal length $d_5$ and coupled by $u \leftrightarrow u + \tau_5 \leftrightarrow \tau_6 - \tau_5 - u$ via the $3\times 3$ constant tridiagonal fiber matrix:

$M_3^{\mathrm{even/odd}}(u) = \begin{pmatrix} V_1(u) & -c_5 & 0 \\ -c_5 & v_2 + c_5 & \pm c_6 \\ 0 & \pm c_6 & v_2 \end{pmatrix}.$

Note that the $(2,2)$ entry on $I_2 = [\tau_5, b]$ is $v_2 + c_5 = c_0 + \tfrac{13}{4}\Delta_\alpha - c_3$ with no $-c_5$ diagonal penalty, because for $x \in [\tau_5, b]$ the only $\tau_5$-shift inside $[-b, b]$ lands in $I_1 = [0, d_5]$ and is already represented exactly by the off-diagonal $-c_5$ entry of $M_3$. Combining the 5 natural-breakpoint prime-reflection step vectors in $+2c_3 P_{\mathrm{anti}}^{(3)} + 2c_5 P_{\mathrm{anti}}^{(5)}$ with the single Plancherel step vector $w_{\star, i} = \mathbf{1}_{m_i < x_6} + (\tfrac{9}{10} - \tfrac{3}{5}\tfrac{m_i - x_6}{b - x_6})\mathbf{1}_{m_i > x_6}$ ($K = 19$) certifies $\mathcal{T}_{\mathrm{even}}(\tfrac{1}{2}\log 7) = 0.99885225 < 1$ and $\mathcal{T}_{\mathrm{odd}}(\tfrac{1}{2}\log 7) = 0.71018432 < 1$.
