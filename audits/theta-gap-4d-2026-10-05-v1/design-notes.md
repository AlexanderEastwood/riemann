# Design plan (internal)

The subject is the missing sign estimate, not a hypothetical discovery of a geometric object. One moving four-coordinate wire surface dominates the page.

Palette: paper #f5f6f4; ink #182c36; observatory #122633; boundary #e7c978; allowed #70ddd0; violation #ff9a89. Georgia body and titles; system sans-serif for controls; monospace only for mathematical expressions. Centered 680px column, left-aligned prose, responsive full-width dark canvas.

Layout: title and precise answer / one model / controls / current inequality / known-versus-missing explanation / provenance. No dashboard metrics or equal-card grid.

Critique before coding: an animated orbit could falsely imply computed theta values. Replace it with a labelled hypothetical local state and camera rotation only. Avoid fabricated trajectory; draw sign-boundary slices and show the required all-height statement as text. The golden wire surface is the content, so remove decorative gradients and badges. Respect reduced motion and provide native keyboard controls.
